# Globex Industries Commute Policy
<!-- company_id: globex | version: v1 | effective_date: 2026-04-01 -->

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose
Globex Industries provides shared cab transport so that employees can reach the office safely and on time. This policy is owned by Human Resources and operated by the transport desk. It describes who can use company transport and the rules that apply.

### 1.2 Applicability
This policy covers all full-time Globex employees based in Globex offices in India. It does not cover employees on deputation to client sites, who follow the client's transport arrangements.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Eligibility criteria
Employees of all grades are eligible for company transport. The employee's registered home address must be more than 8 km from the office by road. Employees living 8 km or closer are not provided transport.

### 2.2 Change of address
Address changes must be submitted to the transport desk at least 5 working days in advance. Eligibility is re-checked against the new address. Routes are re-planned on the 1st and 15th of each month.

### 2.3 Employee registration and verification
An eligible employee must complete the transport registration process before being assigned to a roster. Registration requires the employee identifier, office location, registered residential address, grade, shift, and a reachable contact through the corporate directory. The transport desk verifies the submitted address against the office location and records the verification date. Incomplete registrations remain pending and cannot be used to create a confirmed allocation.

### 2.4 Eligibility review and temporary changes
The transport desk reviews active allocations whenever an employee changes office, shift, or registered residence. A change in work location does not automatically transfer an existing allocation to another office. Employees must submit the revised details at least 5 working days before the intended change. The transport desk confirms the revised eligibility and communicates the effective roster date through the mobile application.

## 3. Night Shift

### 3.1 Shift slot timetable
Transport is planned only for the shift slots below. The drop time is when the cab leaves the office.

| Shift | Login | Logout | Drop departs | Escort for women employees |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | 7:00 AM | 4:00 PM | 4:15 PM | No |
| General | 9:30 AM | 6:30 PM | 6:45 PM | No |
| Mid | 12:00 PM | 9:00 PM | 9:15 PM | No |
| Afternoon | 1:30 PM | 10:30 PM | 11:00 PM | Yes |
| Night | 11:00 PM | 8:00 AM | 8:15 AM | Yes, for the 10:00 PM pickup |

### 3.2 Security escort for women employees
A security escort is provided on drops that leave the office after 10:00 PM when a woman employee is on board. Drops at or before 10:00 PM do not carry an escort. Pickups before 6:00 AM with a woman employee also carry an escort.

The escort is a guard from the security agency contracted by Globex. Women employees can track their cab live in the Globex mobile app and share the trip with a family member. After each night drop, the transport desk calls to confirm that the employee has reached home.

### 3.3 Last drop slot
The last scheduled drop leaves the office at 11:00 PM. No scheduled transport is available after that time.

### 3.4 Shift changes and roster alignment
A shift change must be reflected in the employee's attendance record before the transport desk modifies the transport roster. The employee's reporting manager confirms the revised login and logout timings through the approved workforce system. The transport desk uses the confirmed shift information to determine the applicable pickup or drop slot. An informal request to a driver does not amend an employee's allocation or authorize a different departure time.

### 3.5 Extended work and unscheduled departure
An employee whose work continues beyond the assigned logout time must obtain a separate transport authorization before departure. The authorization records the employee identifier, reporting manager, expected departure, office location, and destination. The scheduled roster remains unchanged until the transport desk confirms a revised allocation. A departure after the final scheduled slot is treated as an ad-hoc requirement and is governed by the approval conditions in Chapter 6.

## 4. Booking

### 4.1 Booking cut-off
Bookings and changes must be made at least 6 hours before the trip through the Globex mobile app. Late requests are not accepted for scheduled trips.

### 4.2 Cancellations
Trips can be cancelled up to 2 hours before the scheduled time. Later cancellations are recorded as no-shows.

### 4.3 Recurring transport allocations
Employees requiring transport on repeated working days must request a recurring allocation through the Globex mobile app. A recurring request may cover no more than 14 calendar days in one submission. The employee must specify the applicable shift, residential boarding point, office destination, and working dates. The transport desk validates the request against the approved roster and confirms each allocation. A recurring request does not override a later change in shift or eligibility.

### 4.4 Booking amendments and confirmation
Every booking amendment must be submitted through the same employee account used for the original request. The application displays a request reference and the current processing status. A request is not confirmed merely because it has been submitted. Employees must check for a confirmed allocation before relying on the revised details. If a confirmation is not displayed within 30 minutes of submission, the employee should contact the transport desk with the request reference.

## 5. Weekends and Holidays

### 5.1 Weekend pickups
Company transport is not provided on Saturdays or Sundays. Employees working on a weekend arrange their own travel.

### 5.2 Public holidays
On holidays that Globex declares as working days, transport runs as on a normal weekday. No transport is provided on other public holidays.

### 5.3 Saturday operational approvals
The standard weekend restriction continues to apply to Saturday work. A department requiring an exceptional Saturday transport arrangement must submit a written business justification to the Director HR at least 2 working days before the proposed service date. Approval must identify the affected employees, office, reporting time, and approved expenditure. The transport desk may arrange a conveyance only after receiving the recorded sanction and confirming vendor capacity. An approved arrangement applies only to the named employees and date.

### 5.4 Sunday and declared holiday operations
Sunday operations require a separate written sanction from the Director HR, even where the department has an approved Saturday arrangement. For a public holiday declared as a working day, the regular weekday roster applies without a separate weekend request. For a public holiday not declared as a working day, scheduled transport is unavailable. Departmental work instructions alone do not create a transport entitlement on a non-working holiday.

## 6. Ad-hoc and Airport Trips

### 6.1 Approval for ad-hoc and airport trips
Ad-hoc trips and airport trips for official travel must be approved by the employee's HR business partner. Approval requests are raised in the Globex mobile app at least one day in advance. Medical emergencies are the only exception and are handled directly by the transport desk.

### 6.2 Airport journey information
An airport request must include the flight departure or arrival time, terminal, employee identifier, reporting location, and requested pickup or drop address. For a departure journey, the employee must provide the flight details and the requested office or residential collection point. For an arrival journey, the request must identify the expected landing time and the destination address. The transport desk checks the requested schedule against the available operating window before confirming the allocation.

### 6.3 Ad-hoc journey limits
An approved ad-hoc journey must have a documented origin and destination. The maximum permitted one-way journey duration is 75 minutes, measured from the recorded pickup time to the final employee drop. The maximum walking distance between an employee's residence and the assigned boarding point is 300 metres. A request exceeding either limit must be referred to the Director HR for an exception decision before a vendor is assigned.

### 6.4 Official airport schedule changes
When an official flight schedule changes after a transport allocation has been confirmed, the employee must update the request in the mobile application immediately after receiving the revised itinerary. The employee should provide the revised flight time and terminal information. The transport desk checks whether the original vendor allocation can be amended. A revised journey is not confirmed until the application displays an updated allocation reference.

## 7. No-shows

### 7.1 No-show policy
An employee who is not at the pickup point within 10 minutes of the cab's arrival is marked as a no-show. Three no-shows in a calendar month suspend the employee's transport for two weeks.

### 7.2 No-show penalty schedule
No-shows are counted per calendar month.

| No-shows in a month | Action | Notified |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logged, no action | None |
| 2 | Reminder message | Employee |
| 3 | Transport suspended for 2 weeks | Employee, HR business partner |
| 4 or more | Referred to HR for review | Employee, HR business partner |

### 7.3 Recording and review of a no-show
The vendor records the vehicle arrival time and the employee's boarding status against the allocated trip reference. The transport desk uses the application record and vendor dispatch record to verify a disputed entry. An employee who believes a no-show was recorded incorrectly must raise a review request within 3 working days of receiving the notification. The review must identify the trip date and reference. The transport desk communicates the outcome through the employee's registered corporate account.

### 7.4 Restoration after suspension
An employee whose allocation has been suspended for two weeks may request restoration after the suspension period ends. The employee must submit the request through the mobile application and acknowledge the applicable boarding requirements. The transport desk checks the suspension record and the employee's current eligibility before restoring access. A pending HR review may delay restoration until the review outcome is recorded. Restoration does not erase the original no-show entries from the calendar-month record.

## 8. Costs

### 8.1 Cost cap
Globex pays up to ₹1,200 for a single ad-hoc trip. Amounts above the cap must be paid by the employee directly to the vendor.

### 8.2 Cost caps by trip type
| Trip type | Cap per trip | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Ad-hoc | ₹1,200 | HR business partner |
| Airport | ₹2,000 | HR business partner |
| Medical emergency | ₹1,500 | Transport desk |
| Late stay beyond last drop | ₹600 | HR business partner |

### 8.3 Cost authorization and supporting records
The applicable trip cap is assessed against the approved journey reference and not against an employee's monthly transport usage. The approving authority must record the trip category and sanctioned amount before the vendor receives the dispatch instruction, except for a medical emergency handled directly by the transport desk. Employees must retain the application confirmation and vendor receipt for any amount they are required to settle. A change in destination requires a revised cost authorization.

### 8.4 Cost treatment for combined journeys
A journey containing multiple destinations must be declared as a combined journey when the request is submitted. The transport desk determines the applicable category from the approved business purpose and records one cost authorization for the complete journey. Employees must not divide a single journey into multiple requests to obtain additional cost limits. If the route is changed after dispatch, the vendor must obtain revised authorization before completing the additional segment.

## 11. Hybrid and Remote Work Transport

### 11.1 Transport entitlement on hybrid workdays
Employees working under an approved hybrid arrangement may use company transport only on dates recorded as office-attendance days in the workforce system. A home-working day does not generate an automatic pickup or drop allocation. Employees must ensure that the office-attendance date and shift are approved before submitting a booking. The transport desk validates the attendance record before confirming the roster. A calendar entry or informal team message is not sufficient evidence of an office-attendance day.

### 11.2 Hybrid-day cancellation deadline
An employee cancelling transport for a hybrid office-attendance day must submit the cancellation at least 24 hours before the scheduled pickup. This deadline applies even when the employee's work arrangement changes after the weekly attendance plan has been published. A cancellation received within the restricted period is recorded against the trip reference for operational review. Employees should verify that the application displays a cancellation confirmation rather than assuming that a submitted request has been withdrawn.

### 11.3 Changes from home-working to office attendance
When an employee changes a planned home-working day to an office-attendance day, the employee must first obtain approval for the attendance change. The transport request may then be submitted through the mobile application, subject to the ordinary 6-hour booking cut-off and available capacity. The transport desk does not treat a changed attendance record as a confirmed conveyance allocation. Employees must wait for the transport confirmation before travelling to the assigned boarding point.

### 11.4 Changes from office attendance to home working
A manager-approved change from office attendance to home working must be reflected in the workforce system. The employee must separately cancel the associated transport allocation through the mobile application. The attendance update alone does not cancel the vendor instruction. The cancellation timestamp is used to determine whether the request met the applicable 24-hour deadline. If the employee's attendance changes after dispatch, the transport desk records the reason and determines the appropriate operational treatment.

### 11.5 Temporary remote-work arrangements
A temporary remote-work arrangement must have a recorded start date, end date, approving manager, and employee identifier. Transport allocations falling within the approved period are reviewed against the revised attendance plan. Employees returning to office attendance must submit their transport request within the applicable booking window. The transport desk does not automatically reactivate a previous allocation when a temporary arrangement expires. A fresh confirmation is required for the first return journey.

### 11.6 Cross-office attendance
An employee attending an office other than the registered base office must obtain approval from the reporting manager and update the work location in the workforce system. The transport desk evaluates eligibility and route capacity for the destination office separately. An existing route allocation cannot be assumed to serve the alternate location. The employee must provide the alternate office address and intended attendance date. Any additional expenditure requires approval under the relevant trip category before dispatch.

### 11.7 Hybrid attendance reconciliation
At the end of each calendar month, the transport desk reconciles confirmed journeys against recorded office-attendance dates. The reconciliation identifies journeys with missing attendance records, duplicate allocations, and cancelled attendance dates. Employees may be asked to provide the relevant request reference or manager approval. The transport desk sends unresolved entries to the reporting manager for confirmation within 5 working days. Reconciliation supports accurate vendor settlement and does not independently alter the employee's eligibility.

## 12. Routes, Zones and Boarding Points

### 12.1 Route planning principles
Routes are designed using verified employee addresses, office destinations, approved shift slots, and available vehicle capacity. The transport desk groups employees with compatible travel directions and departure windows. A route may be revised when address verification, road access, or employee allocation changes. The maximum one-way journey duration is 75 minutes. Route planning must also ensure that the assigned boarding point is no more than 300 metres from the employee's registered residence.

### 12.2 Residential zone classification
For operational planning, the transport desk divides each office catchment into numbered residential zones. Zone assignment is based on road connectivity and the estimated travel time to the office rather than the employee's postal district alone. The zone identifier is displayed in the employee's transport profile. A zone assignment does not guarantee a dedicated vehicle or a particular pickup sequence. The transport desk may combine adjacent zones when the approved route remains within the applicable journey limits.

### 12.3 Boarding point designation
A boarding point is a specific, recorded location where an employee is expected to enter or leave the assigned vehicle. The transport desk selects a location that permits safe vehicle stopping and does not obstruct normal road movement. The employee receives the boarding point description and map marker in the mobile application. Employees must use the confirmed location rather than an unrecorded nearby landmark. A requested change must be assessed before it becomes part of the active roster.

### 12.4 Maximum residential walking distance
The distance between an employee's registered residence and the assigned boarding point must not exceed 300 metres, measured along the pedestrian-accessible approach. The transport desk records the measured distance when approving a new boarding point. A point beyond this limit cannot be assigned through ordinary route planning. The employee may request reassessment where a road closure or access restriction changes the practical approach. The revised location becomes effective only after confirmation in the application.

### 12.5 Journey duration monitoring
The vendor records pickup and final-drop timestamps for each completed journey. The transport desk compares the recorded duration with the 75-minute one-way limit. A journey exceeding the limit is flagged for route review, including examination of additional stops, road restrictions, and dispatch sequencing. Repeated exceedances require a revised route proposal from the vendor within 3 working days. A route cannot be retained solely because its employee allocations have remained unchanged.

### 12.6 Route revision calendar
Regular route revisions are conducted on the 1st and 15th of each month. Address changes received at least 5 working days in advance are considered for the next applicable planning cycle. An operational revision may also be made outside the regular cycle when a route becomes inaccessible or a confirmed safety concern requires relocation. The transport desk communicates the revised route and effective date to affected employees through the mobile application.

### 12.7 Zone-specific dispatch controls
Each route manifest carries a zone identifier, office destination, shift slot, boarding sequence, and assigned vehicle reference. The vendor must follow the approved sequence unless the transport desk authorizes a change. Employees must not independently exchange assigned boarding points with another passenger. If a boarding location becomes inaccessible, the vendor contacts the dispatch coordinator and obtains a revised location before continuing. The updated manifest is retained with the journey record for audit.

## 13. Vehicles and Drivers

### 13.1 Vehicle eligibility and age
Every vehicle assigned to Globex transport must have valid registration, insurance, and statutory fitness documentation. The vehicle must be no more than 4 years old, calculated from its original registration date on the date of assignment. The vendor submits the relevant records before deployment and whenever a document is renewed. The transport desk may reject a vehicle whose documentation is incomplete, expired, or inconsistent with the approved vendor record.

### 13.2 Driver verification
The vendor must submit the driver's identity details, driving licence, employment verification, and police verification record before the driver receives a Globex assignment. The transport desk checks the submitted records against the vendor's approved personnel register. A driver whose verification is incomplete cannot be assigned to an employee journey. Replacement drivers must complete the same verification process before dispatch, including where the replacement is required at short notice.

### 13.3 Driver duty-hour restrictions
A driver may perform no more than 9 duty hours in a calendar day for Globex transport assignments. The vendor is responsible for maintaining duty records covering reporting time, dispatch, breaks, and release from duty. A driver approaching the permitted limit must be replaced through an approved dispatch arrangement. The transport desk may inspect duty records during monthly vendor reviews. An assignment that would exceed the daily limit must not be dispatched.

### 13.4 Vehicle documentation register
The vendor maintains a vehicle register containing the registration identifier, manufacturing year, registration date, insurance validity, fitness certificate expiry, and assigned office. The vendor must notify the transport desk of a change in any recorded detail within 1 working day. The transport desk maintains the verified register and records the date of its latest review. A vehicle with an unresolved documentation discrepancy is removed from the active allocation until the discrepancy is corrected.

### 13.5 Driver assignment and substitution
The driver assigned to a journey must match the identity recorded in the dispatch manifest. If an operational requirement makes substitution necessary, the vendor must submit the replacement driver's verified details to the dispatch coordinator. The coordinator confirms the substitution before the vehicle approaches the first boarding point. The employee's application displays the revised driver information when the dispatch system supports the update. Unverified substitutions are prohibited.

### 13.6 Vehicle cleanliness and passenger readiness
The vendor must present each vehicle in a clean, roadworthy, and passenger-ready condition before the scheduled reporting time. The vehicle interior must be free of loose equipment that could interfere with passenger movement. The driver checks the vehicle's basic operating condition before accepting the dispatch instruction. The transport desk may conduct a physical inspection or request corrective action following an employee report. A vehicle failing inspection must be replaced before the journey begins.

### 13.7 Vendor maintenance and inspection records
Vendors must maintain preventive maintenance records for every assigned vehicle. Records must identify the inspection date, odometer reading, work performed, and the next scheduled inspection. The vendor provides a monthly maintenance summary to the transport desk by the fifth working day of the following month. The transport desk may request supporting records when a vehicle is repeatedly unavailable or receives a safety-related complaint. Unresolved maintenance deficiencies may result in temporary removal from the approved fleet.

## 14. Safety, Incidents and Emergencies

### 14.1 General journey safety
Employees must board only the vehicle and route shown in their confirmed allocation. The driver must verify the employee's identity against the dispatch manifest before recording boarding. Employees must use the available passenger safety restraints and follow reasonable instructions from the driver concerning safe boarding and alighting. The vendor must not add an unapproved passenger or destination to an active journey. The transport desk may suspend a dispatch where a safety requirement cannot be verified.

### 14.2 Incident reporting deadline
Any employee involved in or witnessing a transport incident must notify the transport desk within 1 hour of becoming aware of it. The initial notification may be made through the mobile application or the designated emergency reporting channel. It should include the journey reference, incident time, approximate location, and a brief description. The employee must not delay initial notification while collecting supporting documents. The transport desk records the notification timestamp and assigns an incident reference.

### 14.3 Emergency response and medical expenditure
When a passenger requires immediate medical attention during a journey, the driver must contact emergency services and notify the dispatch coordinator without delaying necessary assistance. The transport desk may authorize emergency medical expenditure up to ₹1,500 per incident. Expenditure beyond this amount requires approval from the Director HR unless a separate statutory requirement applies. The employee or reporting manager should submit available supporting receipts within 5 working days of the incident.

### 14.4 Accident response procedure
Following a road accident, the driver must stop at a safe location where possible, assess immediate danger, and contact emergency services when required. The vendor must notify the transport desk as soon as the situation permits. The transport desk informs the affected employee's emergency contact and reporting manager using the registered records. A replacement conveyance is arranged only after the incident location and passenger condition have been assessed. The vendor must preserve the dispatch manifest and journey timestamps for investigation.

### 14.5 Emergency contact verification
Every registered employee must maintain an emergency contact in the corporate employee record. The contact must include the person's relationship to the employee and a reachable telephone entry. The employee is responsible for reviewing this information whenever a change occurs. The transport desk may use the registered contact when an incident prevents direct communication with the passenger. An outdated contact entry must be referred to the employee for correction within 2 working days.

### 14.6 Incident investigation and corrective action
The transport desk opens an investigation record for each reported incident and assigns an internal reference number. The record contains the initial notification, dispatch manifest, driver statement, available location information, and corrective measures. The vendor must submit its preliminary account within 2 working days. The transport desk reviews responsibility and identifies whether route controls, personnel verification, or vehicle maintenance procedures require modification. Serious incidents are escalated to the Director HR.

### 14.7 Incident closure and employee communication
An incident record may be closed only after the transport desk has documented the investigation outcome and assigned corrective actions. The affected employee receives a closure summary where disclosure is appropriate. Any outstanding vendor action is assigned an owner and target completion date. The transport desk reviews unresolved corrective actions every 7 calendar days until closure. Closing the administrative record does not restrict an employee from pursuing a separate grievance through the applicable internal process.

## 15. Medical and Special Needs Transport

### 15.1 Temporary mobility requirements
An employee with a temporary mobility restriction may request a revised boarding arrangement through the transport desk. The request should state the period for which assistance is required and describe the practical access limitation. Supporting documentation may be requested only to the extent necessary to assess the transport arrangement. The transport desk evaluates available boarding locations, vehicle access, and route feasibility before confirming the change. A temporary arrangement is reviewed when the stated period ends.

### 15.2 Pregnancy-related door-to-door arrangements
A pregnant employee may request a door-to-door drop arrangement from the beginning of the 20th week of pregnancy. The request must identify the employee, shift, registered residence, and expected period of support. The transport desk coordinates with the vendor to determine whether the vehicle can safely access the residential entrance. Where access is restricted, the transport desk records the nearest feasible stopping location and discusses an appropriate alternative with the employee.

### 15.3 Medical appointment transport
Transport for an official medical appointment must be requested as an ad-hoc journey and approved by the HR business partner. The request must identify the appointment date, collection location, destination, and expected return requirement. The applicable ad-hoc approval and cost controls continue to apply. An appointment request does not create an automatic recurring allocation. The transport desk confirms the outbound and return arrangements separately where their timings or destinations differ.

### 15.4 Accessibility assessment
An employee requiring an accessibility-related adjustment may request an assessment without specifying a diagnosis. The transport desk records the functional requirement, such as reduced walking distance or assistance at the boarding point. The assessment considers the route, vehicle access, available boarding space, and the employee's stated requirements. The proposed arrangement is shared with the employee before activation. Any unresolved issue is referred to the Director HR for an administrative decision.

### 15.5 Assistance during boarding and alighting
Where an employee has an approved assistance requirement, the transport manifest must carry the relevant operational instruction without including unnecessary medical information. The vendor must brief the assigned driver before dispatch. The driver must allow adequate time for safe boarding and alighting and notify the dispatch coordinator if the approved arrangement cannot be delivered. The transport desk contacts the employee to agree on an alternative before changing the confirmed boarding location.

### 15.6 Recurring medical transport arrangements
An employee requiring repeated transport for an approved medical purpose may request a recurring arrangement for up to 14 calendar days per submission. Each requested date must be identified in the application, along with the relevant collection and destination details. The transport desk checks route availability and confirms the dates that can be supported. A recurring arrangement is not extended automatically. A fresh request is required when the approved period expires.

### 15.7 Review of special arrangements
Special arrangements are reviewed when the employee's residential address, office location, shift, or stated functional requirement changes. The transport desk records the review date and any agreed modification. The employee is informed before an existing arrangement is discontinued, except where an immediate safety concern requires suspension. A review does not automatically cancel other confirmed journeys. The Director HR decides unresolved administrative exceptions after considering the operational assessment.

## 16. Official and Special Trips

### 16.1 Official client-site journeys
An employee undertaking an approved official visit to a client location must obtain journey authorization from the HR business partner. The request must include the business purpose, destination, travel date, reporting manager, and expected departure time. The transport desk checks the authorization and available vendor capacity before confirming a conveyance. These journeys are treated as special official trips and are not automatically covered by the employee's normal office roster.

### 16.2 Training and development journeys
An employee attending an officially approved training programme at a location other than the base office may request transport through the ad-hoc workflow. The request must include the training approval reference, venue address, programme dates, and reporting schedule. The HR business partner approves the business purpose and applicable expenditure. Transport is confirmed separately for each required journey unless a recurring allocation is explicitly approved within the permitted booking horizon.

### 16.3 Inter-office journeys
A journey between Globex office locations requires a business justification and approval from the employee's reporting manager. The request must identify both office addresses, the participating employees, and the required departure window. The transport desk determines whether the journey can be included in an existing dispatch or requires a separate allocation. The approved destination must be recorded in the journey manifest. A change in destination after confirmation requires fresh authorization.

### 16.4 Conference and corporate event transport
Transport for an officially sanctioned conference or corporate event must be requested by the organizing department. The department must nominate one coordinator who provides the attendee list, venue, event timings, and expected passenger count. The HR business partner validates the business purpose, while the transport desk confirms dispatch capacity and boarding arrangements. Changes to the attendee list must be submitted before the vendor's final manifest is issued.

### 16.5 Airport departure journeys
For an official airport departure, the employee must submit the request at least one day before the journey and obtain HR business partner approval. The request must contain the flight departure time, terminal, pickup address, and intended arrival buffer. The transport desk confirms the allocation after reviewing the requested timing and route duration. The employee must check the confirmed pickup time in the application before the journey date.

### 16.6 Airport arrival journeys
For an official airport arrival, the request must include the flight number reference, expected landing time, terminal, and final destination. The employee must update the application if the flight schedule changes. The transport desk checks whether the existing dispatch can accommodate the revised arrival. A confirmed arrival journey is assigned a collection reference and vendor contact through the application. The employee must not assume that a previous allocation remains valid after a schedule revision.

### 16.7 Special trip completion records
The vendor must submit completion details for each special trip, including the journey reference, actual departure, final arrival, and any approved route deviation. The requesting department verifies the business purpose and passenger list. The transport desk compares the completion record with the sanctioned request before forwarding the entry for billing. An incomplete record is returned to the vendor for correction within 3 working days. The approved request and completion record are retained together.

## 17. Vendors and Service Levels

### 17.1 Vendor appointment and qualification
Globex appoints transport vendors through an administrative qualification process. Each vendor must submit its business registration, statutory transport permits, insurance documentation, vehicle inventory, driver verification process, and operational escalation details. The transport desk evaluates the submitted information before recommending appointment. A vendor cannot receive employee allocations until its qualification is approved and its operating contacts are recorded. Vendor appointment is reviewed annually or whenever a material compliance concern is identified.

### 17.2 On-time pickup performance
The vendor must achieve an on-time pickup performance target of 98% across confirmed Globex journeys in each calendar month. A pickup is considered on time when the vehicle reaches the confirmed boarding point no later than the scheduled pickup time. The transport desk calculates performance using dispatch timestamps and verified journey records. A missed target requires a written corrective action plan from the vendor within 5 working days. Performance is reviewed at the monthly vendor meeting.

### 17.3 Dispatch acknowledgement
The vendor must acknowledge a new confirmed dispatch instruction within 10 minutes of receiving it. The acknowledgement must identify the vehicle registration reference, assigned driver, and expected arrival time. If the vendor cannot fulfil the instruction, it must immediately inform the dispatch coordinator rather than leave the allocation unacknowledged. The transport desk records acknowledgement failures against the vendor's monthly performance report. Repeated failures may result in allocation restrictions or a formal service review.

### 17.4 Replacement vehicle response
When an assigned vehicle becomes unavailable before reaching the first boarding point, the vendor must notify the dispatch coordinator immediately. The vendor should propose a replacement vehicle within 15 minutes of reporting the unavailability. The replacement must satisfy the same documentation and driver-verification requirements as the original assignment. The transport desk informs affected employees of a confirmed substitution through the mobile application. A replacement cannot be treated as approved merely because the vendor has identified a possible vehicle.

### 17.5 Monthly service review
The transport desk conducts a monthly service review covering punctuality, vehicle availability, employee complaints, documentation compliance, incident records, and billing accuracy. The vendor provides the underlying performance report by the fifth working day of the following month. Each unresolved deficiency must have a named vendor owner and a target closure date. The transport desk records the review outcome and communicates material deficiencies to the Director HR. Repeated non-compliance may trigger a formal vendor improvement process.

### 17.6 Service-level deductions
Where the vendor fails to meet a documented service obligation, the transport desk records the affected journey and the applicable contract provision. Any financial deduction is determined under the executed vendor agreement and must be supported by dispatch or incident records. Employees do not independently calculate or collect vendor deductions. The finance team processes an approved adjustment after the transport desk validates the supporting evidence. A disputed deduction is referred to the vendor review process.

### 17.7 Vendor continuity and transition
A vendor transition must be planned so that confirmed employee allocations are not transferred using incomplete records. The outgoing vendor supplies the active route manifests, pending incident references, vehicle records, and unresolved billing entries. The incoming vendor completes qualification and driver verification before receiving any dispatch instruction. The transport desk validates the migrated allocations and communicates the transition date to affected employees at least 3 working days in advance.

## 18. Billing, Charges and Reimbursement

### 18.1 Vendor billing cycle
Vendors submit a consolidated invoice for completed Globex journeys by the third working day of each month. Each invoice must include the journey reference, service date, trip category, approved amount, completion timestamp, and applicable supporting receipt. The transport desk matches invoice entries against confirmed allocations and completion records. Entries without a valid authorization reference are held for clarification. Validated invoices are forwarded to Finance for processing under the vendor agreement.

### 18.2 Billing dispute window
An employee or requesting department must raise a billing dispute within 15 calendar days of the invoice notification or employee charge notification, as applicable. The dispute must identify the journey reference, disputed amount, and reason for disagreement. The transport desk acknowledges the case and checks the original sanction, completion record, and vendor invoice. A dispute submitted after the prescribed window may be declined unless the Director HR records an exception based on documented circumstances.

### 18.3 Employee payment obligations
Where an approved journey exceeds the applicable company cost cap, the employee is responsible for the excess amount. The vendor must disclose the expected employee contribution before completing the journey wherever the excess can reasonably be determined in advance. The employee settles the amount directly with the vendor and obtains a receipt. The transport desk does not reimburse an amount that was neither authorized nor supported by the applicable trip record.

### 18.4 Receipt and charge verification
A vendor receipt must identify the journey date, journey reference, total amount, company-authorized amount, and employee-paid amount where applicable. The employee should compare the receipt with the confirmed request before acknowledging settlement. A missing or inconsistent receipt must be reported to the transport desk within 3 working days. The transport desk may request a corrected receipt from the vendor and retain the correction with the invoice record.

### 18.5 Reimbursement for authorized emergency expenditure
Emergency expenditure personally paid by an employee may be submitted for reimbursement only when the transport desk has recorded the emergency reference and the expenditure falls within the applicable medical emergency cap. The employee must submit the original receipt or a verifiable digital receipt within 5 working days. Finance processes the approved amount after administrative verification. Any amount exceeding the authorized cap requires a separate sanction from the Director HR before reimbursement can be considered.

### 18.6 Duplicate billing and invoice correction
Where the same journey appears more than once in a vendor invoice, the transport desk marks the duplicate entry and requests a corrected invoice. The vendor must provide the correction within 3 working days. Finance must not process the duplicate entry while the correction remains pending. The original and corrected invoice references are retained together to maintain an audit trail. Employees who identify a duplicate employee charge should provide both notification references when raising the dispute.

### 18.7 Monthly financial reconciliation
The transport desk and Finance reconcile the monthly journey register against vendor invoices and recorded employee contributions. The reconciliation identifies completed journeys without invoices, invoiced journeys without completion records, and amounts exceeding approved limits. Differences are assigned to the responsible administrative owner. Unresolved items are carried into the next reconciliation report with their original journey references. The Director HR receives a summary of material unresolved differences at the monthly review.

## 19. Data, Privacy and Tracking

### 19.1 Employee transport information
Globex collects employee information needed to determine eligibility, plan routes, confirm allocations, and administer transport services. This includes the employee identifier, registered residential address, office location, shift, boarding point, and emergency contact. Access is limited to personnel whose responsibilities require the information. The transport desk must not disclose a residential address to another employee merely because both employees share a route. Operational reports should use employee identifiers wherever full personal details are unnecessary.

### 19.2 Location tracking and retention
Journey location data is retained for 180 days from the date of the recorded journey. During this period, access is restricted to authorized transport, security, and investigation personnel with a valid operational purpose. Location records may be used to verify pickup times, investigate an incident, resolve a service complaint, or examine a disputed journey. At the end of the retention period, records are deleted or anonymized according to the approved information-management process, subject to a documented legal preservation requirement.

### 19.3 Employee access to tracking information
An employee may view the live tracking information associated with their own confirmed journey through the Globex mobile application. The application displays the assigned vehicle's journey status and available location information. Tracking access is associated with the employee's authenticated account and is not a general search facility for other employees' journeys. Where tracking information is unavailable, the employee may contact the transport desk using the journey reference. The transport desk records material tracking failures for vendor review.

### 19.4 Access control and administrative review
The transport desk assigns access to transport records according to job responsibility. A person requesting access to an employee's location history must state the operational purpose and obtain authorization from the Director HR or the designated information administrator. Access events are recorded with the requester, date, record category, and reason. Unnecessary copies of employee records must not be retained in personal working folders. Access rights are reviewed every 90 days and whenever a relevant role changes.

### 19.5 Correction of employee records
An employee who identifies an incorrect address, contact detail, shift, or transport profile entry must submit a correction request through the approved employee system. The transport desk verifies the proposed change before updating the active allocation. A correction to residential information may require a fresh eligibility assessment and route review. The previous value is retained only as required for the audit trail and applicable retention controls. The employee receives confirmation when the active profile has been updated.

### 19.6 Disclosure to vendors
A vendor receives only the employee and journey information required to perform an assigned transport service. The dispatch manifest may contain the employee's name or identifier, boarding point, destination, and operational contact instruction. Vendors must not reuse this information for unrelated purposes or share it with unauthorized persons. The vendor must notify the transport desk promptly if an information disclosure or unauthorized access is suspected. The transport desk records the event and refers it for administrative investigation.

### 19.7 Data preservation requests
When an incident, grievance, or formal investigation requires preservation of transport records, the responsible administrator must record the relevant journey references and the reason for preservation. The transport desk identifies the applicable dispatch, location, and vendor records and restricts routine deletion while the preservation instruction remains active. The instruction must specify an owner and review date. Records are released from preservation only after the authorized administrator confirms that the underlying matter has been concluded.

## 20. Feedback, Complaints and Escalation

### 20.1 Employee feedback
Employees may submit service feedback through the Globex mobile application using the relevant journey reference. Feedback may concern route planning, dispatch communication, vehicle readiness, driver conduct, or the accuracy of the assigned boarding point. The transport desk classifies each submission and records its receipt date. A general suggestion that does not identify a particular journey may still be considered for service improvement, but it may not qualify for an individual journey investigation.

### 20.2 Complaint acknowledgement
The transport desk must acknowledge a transport complaint within 4 hours of receipt. The acknowledgement confirms the complaint reference and the team responsible for initial review. It does not constitute a finding on the merits of the complaint. The employee may provide additional supporting information against the same reference. The transport desk maintains the acknowledgement timestamp and monitors the case until it is resolved or formally escalated.

### 20.3 Service-quality complaints
A service-quality complaint concerning punctuality, route sequence, vehicle condition, or dispatch communication is assigned to the transport operations team. The team checks the journey manifest, vendor timestamps, and any available employee submission. The vendor may be asked to provide an explanation within 2 working days. The transport desk communicates the outcome and any corrective measure to the employee. Repeated complaints involving the same route are included in the next monthly vendor review.

### 20.4 Driver conduct grievances
A grievance concerning driver behaviour must be recorded separately from a routine punctuality complaint. The employee should identify the journey reference, approximate time, location, and nature of the reported conduct. The transport desk refers the matter to the security and vendor-management representatives for review. Where an immediate safety concern is alleged, the assigned driver may be removed from further Globex allocations while the initial assessment is conducted. The Director HR reviews serious or repeated allegations.

### 20.5 Complaint escalation stages
The following escalation stages apply to unresolved transport complaints.

| Stage | Responsible role | Escalation trigger | Required record |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Transport Desk | Initial complaint received | Complaint reference |
| 2 | Transport Operations Lead | No resolution after 2 working days | Review summary |
| 3 | Director HR | Material dispute remains unresolved | Escalation decision |
| 4 | Head of Administration | Administrative implementation remains pending | Closure instruction |

### 20.6 Harassment and personal-safety grievances
A complaint involving harassment, intimidation, or personal safety must be referred promptly to the appropriate internal grievance authority. The transport desk records only the minimum journey information needed to identify the incident and preserve supporting records. The complaint must not be circulated through ordinary vendor performance reports. The employee may request that communication be restricted to the designated grievance representatives. Any immediate danger must be addressed through the emergency response process without waiting for the grievance investigation.

### 20.7 Complaint closure and appeal
A complaint is closed after the responsible team records its findings, corrective action, and communication to the employee. An employee who disagrees with the outcome may request a review by the Director HR within 5 working days of receiving the closure communication. The review request must explain which finding or action is disputed. The Director HR may request additional records from the transport desk or vendor before issuing a final administrative decision.

## 21. Weather and Service Disruptions

### 21.1 Weather-based service suspension
Scheduled transport is suspended when the competent local authority issues an orange or red weather alert affecting the operating area. The transport desk checks the alert coverage and expected validity period before issuing a service instruction. Employees are informed through the mobile application and the registered corporate communication channel. The suspension notice identifies the affected office, shift slots, and review time. A department cannot independently instruct a vendor to continue a suspended service.

### 21.2 Road closure and access restrictions
A route may be suspended or diverted when a road closure, public safety instruction, or physical obstruction makes the approved journey impracticable. The vendor must notify the dispatch coordinator with the affected road segment and available alternative. The transport desk assesses whether the revised route remains within the applicable journey limits and boarding controls. Employees receive the updated boarding instruction before dispatch resumes. A diversion that cannot be verified is not treated as an approved route change.

### 21.3 Heavy rainfall and local flooding
Where heavy rainfall causes localized flooding without a qualifying authority alert, the dispatch coordinator assesses the affected road segments using verified operational information. A journey may be delayed, rerouted, or suspended if the route is not safely accessible. The vendor must not enter a visibly inundated road merely to preserve the original pickup schedule. The transport desk records the affected route and the reason for its decision. Employees are informed when a revised service instruction is available.

### 21.4 Public-order restrictions
A transport service may be suspended when a competent authority imposes a movement restriction or the dispatch coordinator receives a verified instruction affecting safe access. The transport desk records the issuing authority, affected area, effective period, and operational instruction. Vendors must comply with the restriction and must not create an alternative route that defeats the stated restriction. The Director HR is informed when a suspension affects multiple offices or an entire shift cycle.

### 21.5 Employee notification during disruption
A disruption notice must identify the affected office, journey category, shift, effective time, and expected next update. Where the full duration is unknown, the notice must state that the service remains under review rather than provide an unsupported restoration estimate. Employees should check the mobile application before proceeding to the assigned boarding point. The transport desk maintains a record of notices issued and the employee groups covered by each notice.

### 21.6 Service restoration
The transport desk restores service after confirming that the relevant alert has been withdrawn or the affected access route is operationally suitable. The vendor must confirm vehicle availability and provide an updated dispatch plan. Restoration is communicated through the application before the first resumed allocation is released. A previously confirmed journey is not automatically reactivated after suspension. Employees must check for a fresh confirmation and follow the revised boarding instruction.

### 21.7 Disruption-related alternative arrangements
Where scheduled transport cannot operate because of an official suspension, the transport desk records the affected employee groups and notifies their reporting managers. Any alternative conveyance requires a separate assessment of safety, availability, and expenditure authorization. An employee must not assume that an ordinary allocation authorizes a different destination or vendor. The Director HR may approve a temporary administrative arrangement where the business requirement and operating conditions justify it.

## 22. Festivals and Peak Days

### 22.1 Festival operating calendar
The transport desk prepares a festival operating calendar using the official Globex working-day calendar and confirmed office attendance requirements. Departments must provide anticipated staffing levels and shift information at least 7 working days before a major festival date. The transport desk uses this information to estimate route demand and vendor capacity. A festival date that is not a declared working day follows the public-holiday restriction in Chapter 5 unless an exception is sanctioned.

### 22.2 Peak-day demand forecast
For a declared working day expected to have unusually high transport demand, departments submit a consolidated attendance forecast to the transport desk. The forecast must identify the office, shift, employee count, and expected pickup window. The transport desk compares projected demand with the approved vendor fleet and records any capacity gap. The resulting dispatch plan is shared with department coordinators at least 2 working days before the operating date.

### 22.3 Festival roster confirmation
Employees must confirm their attendance requirement against the published festival roster. The transport desk issues confirmed boarding details through the mobile application after validating the department's attendance list. An employee omitted from the approved forecast must submit an individual request within the ordinary booking window. Inclusion in a departmental attendance list does not itself guarantee a transport allocation.

### 22.4 Reduced office attendance
When an office operates with reduced staffing on a declared working day, the transport desk may consolidate compatible routes. Consolidation must preserve the applicable journey duration, walking distance, and boarding-point requirements. Employees affected by a revised sequence are notified before dispatch. A route must not be consolidated where doing so would exceed an established operational limit. The transport desk retains the revised manifest and the reason for consolidation.

### 22.5 Peak-day vendor capacity
The vendor must confirm the number of vehicles and verified drivers available for each approved peak-day shift. The confirmation must include any known capacity constraint and a named operational coordinator. The transport desk compares the commitment with the forecast before releasing the final roster. A shortfall is escalated to the Director HR for a decision on priority allocation or a revised operating plan.

### 22.6 Festival-period schedule changes
Where the official working calendar changes after a festival roster has been published, the department coordinator must notify the transport desk immediately. The transport desk updates the attendance basis and reviews the affected allocations. Employees receive a cancellation or revised confirmation through the application. Vendor dispatch instructions must be updated separately. A calendar correction does not automatically withdraw an already issued vehicle instruction.

### 22.7 Post-festival reconciliation
Within 5 working days after a major festival operating period, the transport desk reviews actual passenger counts, cancelled journeys, dispatch failures, and vendor capacity. Departments may be asked to explain material differences between forecast and attendance. The review identifies route changes that should be incorporated into the next planning cycle. Material vendor failures are included in the monthly service review and tracked until corrective action is completed.

## 23. Exceptions and Policy Administration

### 23.1 Exception approval authority
A deviation from the ordinary transport requirements must receive written sanction from the Director HR before implementation. The request must identify the employee or department, applicable policy requirement, business justification, proposed duration, expenditure impact, and operational safeguards. The transport desk records the decision against the relevant request reference. A manager's informal instruction does not constitute an exception approval. Emergency handling remains subject to the specific procedures stated in this policy.

### 23.2 Temporary exception requests
A temporary exception must specify a start date and an end date. The requesting department must explain why ordinary roster or booking arrangements cannot meet the documented requirement. The Director HR may approve, reject, or request additional information before making a decision. An approved exception applies only to the named employee, office, date range, and purpose. It does not create a general entitlement for other employees in the same department.

### 23.3 Permanent policy deviations
A request for a permanent deviation must be supported by a documented operational assessment and an explanation of why a temporary arrangement is insufficient. The Director HR reviews the proposal with Human Resources and the transport desk. If the request changes a general policy requirement, it must be processed as a formal policy amendment rather than maintained as an informal individual exception. Approved amendments receive a version identifier and an effective date.

### 23.4 Emergency administrative decisions
Where an immediate operational situation prevents prior written approval, the transport desk may take only the minimum action needed to protect employee safety or arrange the medical emergency process. The action, reason, affected employee, and expenditure must be recorded as soon as practicable. The Director HR reviews the record on the next working day. This procedure does not create a general waiver of booking, eligibility, or cost controls.

### 23.5 Policy ownership and review
Human Resources owns this policy and is responsible for maintaining its approved version. The transport desk administers day-to-day operations and proposes amendments based on operational evidence. The policy is reviewed at least once every 12 months or earlier when a material regulatory or operational change occurs. Proposed changes must be documented with their rationale and impact. Only the authorized policy owner may issue a revised controlled version.

### 23.6 Interpretation of conflicting instructions
Where a departmental instruction conflicts with an approved transport allocation or this policy, the transport desk refers the issue to Human Resources for interpretation. The more specific safety requirement applies while clarification is pending. Employees and vendors must not treat an unverified message as a formal policy amendment. The interpretation and resulting instruction are recorded against the affected journey or policy reference to preserve an auditable decision trail.

### 23.7 Policy communication and employee acknowledgement
Human Resources publishes the current policy through the approved employee information system. Employees must acknowledge the applicable transport rules when registering for company transport and when a material revision is issued. The transport desk may direct employees to the relevant policy section when resolving a request. Acknowledgement confirms receipt of the policy and does not replace any separate approval or booking requirement.

## 24. Roles and Contacts

### 24.1 Human Resources
Human Resources owns the policy, coordinates formal amendments, and provides administrative interpretation. It ensures that the transport framework remains aligned with approved employee processes. Human Resources reviews material escalations referred by the transport desk and records decisions that affect general policy administration. The policy owner also ensures that employees can access the current controlled version. Operational dispatch decisions remain with the transport desk unless a specific approval is required.

### 24.2 Transport Desk
The transport desk manages employee registration, route planning, booking validation, vendor dispatch, journey monitoring, complaint intake, and billing reconciliation. It maintains the official transport roster and confirms changes through the mobile application. The desk records operational exceptions and escalates matters requiring formal sanction. Employees should provide their journey reference when requesting assistance so that the correct dispatch and allocation records can be retrieved.

### 24.3 HR Business Partner
The HR business partner reviews business-purpose requests for ad-hoc journeys, airport journeys, and applicable special trips. The role confirms that the request is connected to an approved employee or business requirement and that the required details are present. The HR business partner does not independently dispatch a vehicle. Once the approval is recorded, the transport desk validates operational capacity and issues the confirmed allocation.

### 24.4 Director HR
The Director HR provides the formal exception sanction required by this policy and reviews serious escalations. The role may approve a documented deviation, reject it, or request a revised proposal with additional safeguards. The Director HR also reviews unresolved grievances and material vendor-performance concerns. Decisions must be recorded with the applicable employee, department, request reference, effective period, and any conditions attached to the sanction.

### 24.5 Reporting Manager
The reporting manager confirms an employee's approved shift and office-attendance requirement through the workforce system. The manager also validates business-purpose information for special journeys and departmental transport forecasts. A reporting manager cannot independently change an employee's transport allocation or waive a policy limit. Where a manager identifies a recurring operational need, the requirement must be submitted through the appropriate approval channel.

### 24.6 Vendor Dispatch Coordinator
The vendor dispatch coordinator manages vehicle assignment, driver confirmation, dispatch acknowledgement, route execution, and operational substitutions. The coordinator communicates service failures and journey incidents to the transport desk. The coordinator must follow the approved manifest and cannot independently alter an employee's destination or boarding point. All material dispatch changes must be supported by an updated instruction and retained in the vendor's operating record.

### 24.7 Employee responsibilities
Employees must maintain accurate registration information, submit requests within the applicable booking window, and use only confirmed allocations. They must verify the assigned boarding point and report material discrepancies to the transport desk. Employees must also raise incident notifications and complaints through the designated channels. An employee is responsible for checking that a submitted request has reached confirmed status before relying on the requested journey.
