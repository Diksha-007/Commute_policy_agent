# Initech Solutions Transport Guidelines
<!-- company_id: initech | version: v1 | effective_date: 2026-02-15 -->

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose
These guidelines explain the transport facility Initech Solutions offers to its employees. The Facilities team owns the guidelines and the transport desk runs day-to-day operations. The goal is a safe and reliable commute for employees whose roles need it most.

### 1.2 Applicability
The guidelines apply to Initech employees working from Initech offices in India, including employees on fixed-term contracts. Consultants and vendor staff are not covered.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Eligibility criteria
Only employees in grades L1 to L3 are eligible for company transport, at any distance from the office. Employees in grade L4 and above are not eligible.

### 2.2 Change of address
Employees must update their address in the HR system before their next roster is published. The transport desk picks up the change from the next roster.

### 2.3 Employee registration and verification
Eligible employees must complete transport registration before their names can be included in a published roster. Registration requires the employee identifier, employment category, grade, office location, registered residential address, and assigned shift. The transport desk verifies the employee's grade against the HR system and checks that the employee is associated with an operating Initech office. A pending verification prevents a new allocation from being confirmed.

### 2.4 Grade changes and eligibility review
Eligibility is determined using the employee's grade recorded in the HR system when the next roster is prepared. An employee promoted from L3 to L4 or above becomes ineligible for new allocations after the updated grade is reflected in the roster. A change to a lower eligible grade may be considered in the next roster cycle. The transport desk must not independently override a grade recorded by Human Resources.

### 2.5 Employment status and temporary assignment
Fixed-term employees remain within the scope of these guidelines while their employment status is active and their grade falls within L1 to L3. The employee's recorded contract end date is checked when preparing future allocations. A temporary change in team or reporting manager does not independently change transport eligibility. Employees whose employment status is pending verification must obtain confirmation from Human Resources before a new allocation is issued.

## 3. Night Shift

### 3.1 Shift slot timetable
Transport is planned only for the shift slots below. The drop time is when the cab leaves the office.

| Shift | Login | Logout | Drop departs | Escort for women employees |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Early | 6:30 AM | 3:30 PM | 3:45 PM | No |
| General | 9:00 AM | 6:00 PM | 6:15 PM | No |
| Late | 12:00 PM | 9:00 PM | 9:15 PM | Yes |
| Afternoon | 1:00 PM | 10:00 PM | 10:30 PM | Yes |
| Night | 9:30 PM | 6:30 AM | 6:45 AM | Yes, for the 8:30 PM pickup |

### 3.2 Security escort for women employees
Drops that leave the office after 9:00 PM with a woman employee on board carry a security escort. Pickups before 6:00 AM with a woman employee on board also carry an escort.

Women employees may ask the transport desk to change their drop order on a route. Drivers on night routes must have completed the Initech safety briefing.

### 3.3 Last drop slot
The last scheduled drop leaves the office at 10:30 PM. Employees who stay later need an ad-hoc trip under section 6.

### 3.4 Shift allocation and roster changes
The transport desk prepares employee allocations using the shift information recorded in the HR system. A manager-approved shift change must be entered into the system before the next roster is finalized. Employees must check the published allocation after a change rather than relying on an earlier shift assignment. A driver cannot authorize an employee to move to another shift route. Any urgent requirement arising after publication must be referred to the transport desk for an operational decision.

### 3.5 Night-route driver briefing
Every driver assigned to a night route must complete the Initech safety briefing before receiving the assignment. The vendor maintains a completion register containing the driver's identifier, briefing date, and confirmation status. The transport desk checks the register before accepting a new night-route driver. A replacement driver must meet the same requirement. A vendor's assurance that a briefing will be completed later is not sufficient for dispatch authorization.

## 4. Booking

### 4.1 Booking cut-off
Same-day drops must be booked by 3:00 PM on the day of the trip. Pickups for the next morning must be booked by 3:00 PM the previous day.

### 4.2 Cancellations
Trips can be cancelled up to 30 minutes before the scheduled time. Later cancellations count as no-shows.

### 4.3 Recurring booking horizon
An employee requiring transport on repeated working days must submit a recurring booking request for no more than 7 calendar days at a time. The request must specify the working dates, shift, residential address, office destination, and required pickup or drop arrangement. The transport desk validates the request against the employee's eligibility and published roster. A recurring request does not create an allocation for dates outside the approved period. Employees must submit another request when the existing period expires.

### 4.4 Booking confirmation and amendments
The transport desk records each accepted booking against an employee identifier and journey reference. Employees must check the application or the published roster to confirm that the request has been accepted. A submitted request is not a confirmed allocation. Changes to the date, shift, boarding point, or destination must be communicated before the applicable booking deadline. An amendment is effective only after the transport desk confirms the revised allocation.

### 4.5 Same-day booking administration
Same-day drop requests must reach the transport desk by 3:00 PM on the date of the proposed journey. The desk checks the employee's shift, eligibility, and available vehicle capacity before confirming the request. Requests received after the stated deadline are not treated as ordinary scheduled bookings. Employees must not assume that a vehicle can be retained at the office merely because their work is continuing beyond the published logout time.

### 4.6 Next-morning pickup administration
A pickup required on the following morning must be requested no later than 3:00 PM on the preceding day. The transport desk uses the published shift and route roster to determine the pickup allocation. Requests received after the deadline are referred for operational review and are not automatically accepted. Employees must verify the final boarding instruction before the journey date. A previously confirmed pickup cannot be transferred to a different date without a recorded amendment.

### 4.7 Booking record retention
The transport desk maintains the booking reference, employee identifier, requested journey date, confirmed shift, approval status, and cancellation status. These records are used to investigate allocation discrepancies and reconcile vendor invoices. The desk may request the employee's confirmation reference when reviewing a disputed booking. Changes must be traceable to the person or system that submitted them. An unrecorded verbal arrangement does not replace the official booking record.

## 5. Weekends and Holidays

### 5.1 Weekend pickups
Weekend transport on Saturday and Sunday is available only to shift workers who are rostered to work that day. Other employees are not provided weekend transport.

### 5.2 Public holidays
On public holidays, transport is provided only to shift workers on the published roster.

### 5.3 Saturday shift-worker transport
A Saturday allocation is available only where the employee is a shift worker and the Saturday duty appears on the published roster. The shift manager confirms the work requirement before the transport desk includes the employee in the weekend manifest. Employees working additional hours without a corresponding roster entry do not receive an automatic weekend allocation. A revised shift roster must be recorded before transport is confirmed. The transport desk checks vendor capacity and communicates the final boarding details.

### 5.4 Sunday shift-worker transport
Sunday transport follows the same eligibility principle but requires a separate confirmation against the Sunday duty roster. The employee's shift manager must verify the Sunday assignment and provide the confirmed employee list to the transport desk. A Saturday allocation does not automatically carry over to Sunday. The desk checks the Sunday manifest independently and confirms the relevant journey references. An employee who is not included in the published Sunday roster must not rely on a previous weekend allocation.

### 5.5 Public holiday roster verification
On a public holiday, transport is limited to shift workers whose duty appears on the published roster. The shift manager submits the confirmed list to the transport desk before the holiday dispatch plan is finalized. The employee's shift and attendance date must match the approved record. A general department instruction does not independently establish eligibility for holiday transport. The transport desk records the approved manifest and communicates any route changes to affected employees.

### 5.6 Holiday roster amendments
A change to a holiday duty assignment must be reflected in the approved roster before transport can be added or removed. The shift manager communicates the amendment to the transport desk with the employee identifier and revised duty timing. The desk checks whether the existing vendor allocation can accommodate the change. An amended allocation is effective only after confirmation. A change in the employee's work attendance does not automatically modify a vehicle instruction already issued to the vendor.

## 6. Ad-hoc and Airport Trips

### 6.1 Approval for ad-hoc and airport trips
Ad-hoc trips and airport trips for official travel must be approved by the head of the employee's department. The approved request is forwarded to the transport desk by email.

### 6.2 Ad-hoc request particulars
An ad-hoc request must identify the employee, department, business purpose, date, pickup location, destination, and expected departure time. The department head's approval must be included in the request forwarded to the transport desk. The desk checks the details and available operating capacity before confirming a vehicle. An incomplete request is returned for correction. A request sent directly by an employee without the required departmental approval is not treated as an authorized dispatch instruction.

### 6.3 Airport journey particulars
An airport request must identify whether the journey is for departure or arrival, the flight reference, terminal, relevant flight time, collection address, and final destination. The head of the employee's department approves the official travel requirement. The approved request is forwarded to the transport desk by email. The desk checks the requested timing against the journey schedule and confirms the allocation separately. An itinerary change must be communicated as soon as it becomes known.

### 6.4 Ad-hoc journey duration
The maximum permitted one-way journey duration is 120 minutes, measured from the recorded pickup time to the final drop. The transport desk assesses the proposed journey before confirming the vendor assignment. A route expected to exceed this limit requires a documented exception from the Facilities Manager. The vendor must report material delays that are likely to cause an approved journey to exceed the limit. The desk records the reason and the final journey duration for operational review.

### 6.5 Boarding-point distance
The maximum walking distance between an employee's registered residential location and the assigned boarding point is 800 metres. The transport desk considers pedestrian access and the practical route when designating the boarding location. A point exceeding this limit must be reviewed before it is included in the roster. Employees may request reassessment when a road closure or access restriction makes the recorded approach unsuitable. A revised boarding point becomes effective only after the desk confirms the change.

### 6.6 Late departure after the final scheduled slot
An employee whose work continues beyond the final scheduled drop at 10:30 PM must obtain an approved ad-hoc journey. The employee's department head must approve the business requirement and forward the request to the transport desk. The desk checks the departure time, destination, available vehicle, and applicable expenditure limit. The ordinary scheduled roster does not authorize an additional departure. Employees must wait for a confirmed allocation before proceeding with the journey.

### 6.7 Official trip completion
The vendor records the actual pickup time, final arrival time, employee reference, and journey completion status for every approved special trip. The transport desk compares the completion record with the departmental approval before accepting the invoice entry. A material change in destination or journey purpose must be referred for revised approval. The requesting department may be asked to confirm the business purpose where the completion record differs from the original request.

## 7. No-shows

### 7.1 No-show policy
An employee who is not at the pickup point within 5 minutes of the cab's arrival is marked as a no-show. The first no-show in a calendar month results in a warning. The second no-show in the same month carries a fine of ₹500.

### 7.2 No-show penalty schedule
No-shows are counted per calendar month.

| No-shows in a month | Action | Notified |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warning | Employee |
| 2 | ₹500 fine | Employee, department head |
| 3 | Transport suspended for 1 month | Employee, department head |
| 4 or more | Referred to HR | Employee, department head, HR |

### 7.3 No-show recording procedure
The vendor records the vehicle arrival time and the employee's boarding status against the confirmed journey reference. The transport desk uses the dispatch record to determine whether the vehicle reached the designated pickup point. The five-minute attendance window is measured from the recorded arrival time. The employee's absence is recorded against the relevant calendar month. A driver statement alone does not replace the dispatch record when a no-show is disputed.

### 7.4 No-show dispute submission
An employee disputing a no-show must submit a written review request within 3 working days of receiving the relevant notification. The request must include the journey reference, travel date, and reason for disputing the record. The transport desk checks the dispatch timestamp and vendor manifest before making a recommendation. Where the record is inconsistent, the entry is referred for correction. A dispute does not automatically suspend the original administrative action while the review is pending.

### 7.5 Monthly count and penalty application
No-shows are counted separately for each calendar month. The first recorded occurrence results in a warning, while the second carries a charge of ₹500. A third occurrence results in a one-month transport suspension. A fourth or subsequent occurrence is referred to Human Resources and the department head for review. The transport desk verifies the monthly count before applying an action. A new calendar month begins a fresh counting period.

### 7.6 Suspension and reinstatement
An employee suspended after the third no-show may request reinstatement after the one-month suspension period has ended. The request must be submitted to the transport desk and must identify the employee and relevant suspension record. The desk verifies the suspension period and current eligibility before restoring the employee's allocation. A pending HR review may require a separate administrative decision. Reinstatement does not erase the no-show history for the month in which the events occurred.

### 7.7 No-show notification records
The transport desk records the employee identifier, journey reference, date, recorded arrival time, no-show count, and administrative action. The employee receives the applicable notification through the registered employee communication channel. Where a department head or HR notification is required, the desk records the date and recipient role. A correction to the no-show record must retain the original entry and the reason for the correction so that the monthly record remains auditable.

## 8. Costs

### 8.1 Cost cap
Initech pays up to ₹1,000 for a single ad-hoc trip. Any excess is billed to the employee's department.

### 8.2 Cost caps by trip type

| Trip type | Cap per trip | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Ad-hoc | ₹1,000 | Department head |
| Airport | ₹1,800 | Department head |
| Weekend (shift workers) | ₹900 | Shift manager |
| Guest | ₹2,500 | Department head |

### 8.3 Cost authorization
Every non-scheduled journey must have a recorded cost authorization before the transport desk issues the vendor instruction. The approval must identify the journey category, employee or host department, expected expenditure, and approving role. The transport desk checks the applicable cap before confirming the request. An amount exceeding the relevant cap is assigned to the employee's department where the guidelines so provide. A vendor cannot independently approve an increase to the sanctioned amount.

### 8.4 Departmental excess charges
Any amount above the ₹1,000 ad-hoc trip cap is billed to the employee's department. The transport desk records the approved company contribution and the excess amount separately. The department head receives the supporting journey reference and vendor charge record for verification. The department must identify any disputed amount within the applicable billing review period. An employee is not personally assigned an excess ad-hoc charge under the ordinary cost rule.

### 8.5 Airport trip expenditure
The company cost cap for an approved airport journey is ₹1,800 per trip. The department head approves the official travel request and the transport desk validates the expected expenditure before dispatch. A journey expected to exceed the cap must be referred for an administrative decision before confirmation. The final vendor invoice must identify the airport journey reference and the approved amount. A changed terminal or destination must be reflected in the revised journey record.

### 8.6 Weekend transport expenditure
The maximum company cost cap for a weekend journey assigned to a shift worker is ₹900 per trip. The shift manager approves the rostered work requirement and the transport desk confirms the corresponding allocation. The vendor must identify the journey as a weekend shift-worker service in its completion record. Any proposed expenditure above the cap requires a separate administrative decision before dispatch. A weekend allocation cannot be reclassified as an ordinary ad-hoc journey merely to obtain another cost limit.

### 8.7 Cost record reconciliation
The transport desk compares the vendor invoice with the approved journey category, cost cap, and completion record. A discrepancy is referred to the vendor or approving department for clarification. The record must identify the amount authorized by the company and any excess assigned to the department. Finance processes only the validated invoice entry. A correction to an invoice must retain the original reference and the reason for the amendment.

## 9. Guest Travel

### 9.1 Guest travel
Guest travel is allowed. The cost of each guest trip is billed to the host employee's department, and the department head approves it.

### 9.2 Guest journey request
A guest journey must be requested by a host employee who is responsible for providing the guest's name, visit purpose, journey date, collection location, and destination. The department head must approve the request before it is forwarded to the transport desk. The host employee remains responsible for confirming the guest's expected arrival and ensuring that the guest has the correct boarding information. The transport desk records the host employee and department against the journey reference.

### 9.3 Guest journey cost authorization
The maximum cost cap for a guest journey is ₹2,500 per trip. The host employee's department bears the cost of the approved journey, and the department head must approve the expenditure. The transport desk checks the expected charge against the cap before issuing the vendor instruction. An amount above the cap requires a separate documented decision before dispatch. The guest is not treated as an eligible employee merely because the journey is hosted by an employee.

### 9.4 Guest identification and dispatch
The host employee must provide the guest's name and the minimum identification details required for the vendor to identify the authorized passenger. The transport desk records the guest journey separately from employee roster allocations. The driver receives the approved pickup point and destination through the dispatch manifest. A guest must not be added to an employee's existing route without confirmation from the transport desk and approval under the guest travel process.

### 9.5 Host responsibility
The host employee is responsible for submitting accurate journey details and informing the guest of the confirmed collection instructions. If the guest's schedule changes, the host must notify the transport desk and request an amendment. The original allocation remains applicable until a revised instruction is confirmed. The host employee must inform the department head if the revised journey affects the approved expenditure. The transport desk records material changes against the guest journey reference.

### 9.6 Guest cancellation and non-boarding
A guest journey may be cancelled up to 30 minutes before its scheduled time. A cancellation received after that point is recorded against the host employee's department for administrative review. If a guest does not arrive at the confirmed pickup point, the vendor records the arrival time and boarding status. The transport desk informs the host employee and retains the record for invoice verification. The guest journey is not counted as an employee no-show.

## 11. Hybrid and Remote Work Transport

### 11.1 Office attendance requirement
Employees working under an approved hybrid arrangement may receive transport only for dates on which they are recorded as attending an Initech office. A home-working day does not create an automatic transport allocation. The employee's manager must ensure that the attendance record is updated before the transport desk prepares the applicable roster. The employee must check the confirmed journey details before travelling to the assigned boarding point.

### 11.2 Hybrid-day cancellation deadline
A transport allocation for a hybrid office-attendance day must be cancelled at least 6 hours before the scheduled journey. The cancellation timestamp in the booking record determines whether the deadline was met. Employees must submit the cancellation through the approved transport channel. A change to the attendance calendar does not independently withdraw a vendor instruction. The transport desk records a late cancellation against the relevant journey reference.

### 11.3 Change from remote work to office attendance
When an employee changes a planned remote-working day to office attendance, the employee must obtain manager approval and update the attendance record. A transport request must then be submitted within the ordinary booking window. The transport desk checks the employee's grade, shift, and route availability before confirming an allocation. An attendance update does not itself establish that a vehicle has been assigned. The employee must wait for a confirmed journey reference.

### 11.4 Change from office attendance to remote work
When a manager approves a change from office attendance to remote work, the employee must separately cancel the associated transport booking. The cancellation is processed using the applicable 6-hour deadline. The transport desk checks the booking record and updates the vendor dispatch instruction where necessary. If the vendor has already commenced the journey, the desk records the operational circumstances and determines the appropriate treatment under the booking and cost rules.

### 11.5 Temporary remote-work periods
A temporary remote-work arrangement must identify the approved start date, end date, employee, and approving manager. Transport allocations within the period are reviewed against the revised attendance record. Employees returning to office attendance must submit a new request within the relevant booking window. The expiry of a remote-work arrangement does not automatically restore a previous route allocation. The transport desk confirms the first return journey separately.

### 11.6 Alternate office attendance
An employee reporting to an alternate Initech office must have the destination office recorded in the HR system. The transport desk assesses the revised route and vehicle capacity before issuing an allocation. An existing route cannot be assumed to serve another office. The employee's grade and shift eligibility remain subject to Chapter 2. Any additional expenditure must be approved under the relevant trip category before dispatch.

## 12. Routes, Zones and Boarding Points

### 12.1 Route planning principles
The transport desk prepares routes using employee addresses, eligible grades, shift timings, office destinations, and available vehicle capacity. The maximum one-way journey duration is 120 minutes. Route planning must also respect the maximum 800-metre walking distance between an employee's residence and the designated boarding point. Routes are revised when an address, operating condition, or approved allocation changes. The desk records the effective date of each revised route.

### 12.2 Residential zone classification
The transport desk groups residential locations into operating zones based on road connectivity and estimated journey time. Each zone is assigned an internal identifier that appears in the route manifest. Zone membership does not guarantee a dedicated vehicle or a fixed boarding sequence. Adjacent zones may be combined where the revised journey remains within the prescribed duration and walking limits. The transport desk retains the basis for material zone changes.

### 12.3 Boarding-point designation
A boarding point is the recorded location at which an employee is expected to enter or leave the assigned vehicle. The transport desk selects a location that permits safe stopping and practical pedestrian access. The location is communicated through the published roster or the approved employee communication channel. Employees must use the confirmed boarding point unless the transport desk approves a change. A nearby landmark is not an alternative boarding point until it is formally recorded.

### 12.4 Maximum walking distance
The walking distance from an employee's registered residential location to the designated boarding point must not exceed 800 metres. The transport desk assesses the pedestrian-accessible approach rather than relying solely on straight-line distance. A proposed boarding point exceeding the limit must be revised or referred for an exception decision by the Facilities Manager. Employees may request reassessment where a physical obstruction changes the practical route. The revised point takes effect after confirmation.

### 12.5 Journey duration monitoring
The vendor records the pickup timestamp and final drop timestamp for each journey. The transport desk checks completed journeys against the 120-minute one-way limit. An exceedance is flagged for examination of route sequencing, road access, dispatch delays, and additional stops. The vendor must submit an explanation for repeated exceedances and propose a corrective route plan. The transport desk retains the review outcome with the relevant route manifest.

### 12.6 Route revision and employee notification
A route revision may be required after an employee address change, office transfer, shift amendment, or operational access restriction. The transport desk updates the route manifest and identifies the employees affected by the change. Employees receive the revised boarding location and journey details before the change becomes effective. A revised allocation is not valid merely because a vendor has changed its internal dispatch sheet. The official roster must reflect the updated arrangement.

### 12.7 Route deviation authorization
The driver must follow the approved route unless an operational restriction requires an immediate safe diversion. The vendor must notify the dispatch coordinator of the reason, affected road segment, and proposed alternative. The transport desk checks whether the deviation remains within the journey limits and updates the route record. A material change in destination requires fresh authorization. Unrecorded deviations are reviewed during the monthly vendor reconciliation.

### 12.8 Boarding-point access review
Employees may report a boarding location that has become inaccessible because of a road closure, construction activity, or another physical obstruction. The transport desk checks the reported condition and evaluates an alternative within the prescribed walking distance. The vendor must not independently move the boarding point without an approved instruction. The employee receives the revised location and effective date through the official communication channel. Temporary changes are reviewed when the access restriction ends.

## 13. Vehicles and Drivers

### 13.1 Vehicle eligibility and age
Every vehicle assigned to Initech transport must have valid registration, insurance, and statutory fitness documentation. The maximum permitted vehicle age is 7 years, calculated from the original registration date on the date of assignment. The vendor submits the supporting records before deployment and whenever a document is renewed. The transport desk may reject a vehicle with expired or inconsistent documentation.

### 13.2 Driver verification
The vendor must submit driver identity details, a valid driving licence, employment verification, and the required police verification record before assigning a driver. The transport desk checks the submitted information against the approved vendor personnel register. An individual whose verification is incomplete cannot receive an Initech journey assignment. Replacement drivers must complete the same verification requirements before dispatch, including for urgent substitutions.

### 13.3 Driver duty-hour restriction
A driver may perform no more than 12 duty hours in a calendar day for Initech transport assignments. The vendor maintains records of reporting time, assigned journeys, breaks, and release from duty. A driver approaching the permitted limit must be replaced through a verified dispatch arrangement. The transport desk may inspect the duty register during vendor reviews. An assignment expected to exceed the daily limit must not be dispatched.

### 13.4 Vehicle documentation register
The vendor maintains a register identifying each vehicle's registration number, original registration date, insurance validity, fitness certificate expiry, and assigned operating office. The vendor notifies the transport desk of changes within 1 working day. The desk maintains the verified register and records the latest review date. A vehicle with an unresolved documentation discrepancy is excluded from active assignments until the issue has been corrected.

### 13.5 Driver substitution
A replacement driver must satisfy the same verification and safety briefing requirements as the original assigned driver. The vendor submits the replacement's identity and licence details to the dispatch coordinator before the substitution takes effect. The coordinator checks the details and updates the journey manifest. The employee receives the revised assignment information through the available communication channel. An unverified replacement cannot be treated as an authorized driver.

### 13.6 Vehicle inspection and readiness
The vendor must ensure that each assigned vehicle is roadworthy and ready before its scheduled reporting time. The driver completes a basic pre-dispatch inspection and reports defects to the vendor coordinator. The transport desk may request supporting inspection records following a reported concern. A vehicle with a safety-related defect must be removed from the assignment until the vendor confirms corrective action. The replacement vehicle must independently satisfy the documentation requirements.

### 13.7 Vehicle maintenance records
The vendor retains preventive maintenance records identifying inspection dates, odometer readings, maintenance activity, and the next inspection date. A monthly summary is submitted to the transport desk by the fifth working day of the following month. The desk may request additional records following a breakdown or repeated service failure. Maintenance deficiencies must be assigned a corrective action and target completion date. Unresolved deficiencies may result in temporary removal from the approved fleet.

## 14. Safety, Incidents and Emergencies

### 14.1 General passenger safety
Employees must use only the vehicle and journey allocation confirmed by the transport desk. The driver checks the passenger against the dispatch manifest before recording boarding. Employees must use the installed passenger safety restraints and follow reasonable instructions relating to safe entry and exit. The vendor must not add an unauthorized passenger or destination to an active journey. The transport desk may stop a dispatch when a material safety requirement cannot be verified.

### 14.2 Incident reporting deadline
An employee involved in or witnessing a transport incident must notify the transport desk within 24 hours of becoming aware of it. The initial report must identify the journey reference, approximate incident time, location, and a brief description. Notification should be made through the approved employee reporting channel. Supporting documents may follow after the initial report. The transport desk records the receipt time and assigns an incident reference for investigation.

### 14.3 Medical emergency expenditure
Where a passenger requires immediate medical assistance during a journey, the driver must contact emergency services and notify the dispatch coordinator without delaying necessary assistance. The transport desk may authorize medical emergency expenditure up to ₹2,000 per incident. An amount above this cap requires approval from the Facilities Manager unless an overriding legal obligation applies. Available receipts and supporting records must be submitted within 5 working days.

### 14.4 Accident response
Following a road accident, the driver must stop at a safe location where practicable, assess immediate danger, and contact emergency services when required. The vendor informs the transport desk as soon as the circumstances permit. The desk contacts the affected employee's emergency contact and reporting manager using the registered records. A replacement conveyance is arranged after the passenger situation and incident location have been assessed. The dispatch manifest and available journey records are preserved.

### 14.5 Emergency contact verification
Every registered employee must maintain an emergency contact in the HR system. The contact record must identify the person and the relationship to the employee. Employees are responsible for correcting outdated information. The transport desk may use the registered contact when an incident prevents direct communication with the passenger. A contact discrepancy identified during registration must be referred to the employee before the transport profile is finalized.

### 14.6 Incident investigation
The transport desk opens an investigation record for each reported incident and assigns an internal reference. The record includes the initial report, journey manifest, available driver statement, relevant dispatch timestamps, and corrective measures. The vendor provides a preliminary account within 3 working days of the request. The Facilities team reviews material findings and determines whether route, vehicle, or personnel controls require modification. Serious cases are referred to Human Resources.

### 14.7 Incident closure
An incident record is closed after the transport desk documents the findings, corrective actions, and communication to the affected employee where appropriate. Every outstanding corrective action must have an assigned owner and target completion date. The desk reviews unresolved actions every 7 calendar days until closure. Administrative closure does not prevent an employee from submitting a separate grievance under the applicable internal process.

### 14.8 Emergency escalation
An immediate threat to passenger safety must be referred to emergency services and the transport desk without waiting for ordinary administrative approval. The dispatch coordinator informs the Facilities team when an incident affects multiple passengers or a route. The Facilities Manager reviews the operational response and authorizes necessary administrative measures. The incident record must identify the action taken, the person responsible, and the reason that normal procedures could not be followed.

## 15. Medical and Special Needs Transport

### 15.1 Temporary mobility requirements
An employee with a temporary mobility limitation may request a revised boarding arrangement from the transport desk. The request should describe the practical assistance required and the period for which it is needed. The desk evaluates the route, vehicle access, and available boarding locations before confirming a modification. Supporting information may be requested only where necessary to assess the arrangement. Temporary adjustments are reviewed when the specified period ends.

### 15.2 Pregnancy-related door-to-door transport
A pregnant employee may request a door-to-door drop arrangement from the beginning of the 28th week of pregnancy. The request must identify the employee, shift, registered residential address, and expected support period. The transport desk coordinates with the vendor to assess whether the vehicle can safely reach the residential entrance. If access is restricted, the desk identifies the nearest feasible stopping location and discusses an alternative with the employee.

### 15.3 Medical appointment journeys
Transport for an official medical appointment must be requested as an ad-hoc journey and approved by the employee's department head. The request must include the appointment date, collection point, destination, and expected return requirement. The applicable ad-hoc booking and expenditure controls continue to apply. An appointment request does not automatically create a recurring allocation. The transport desk confirms the outbound and return arrangements separately where the timing or destination differs.

### 15.4 Accessibility assessment
An employee requiring an accessibility-related adjustment may request an assessment without disclosing unnecessary medical details. The transport desk records the functional requirement, such as a reduced walking distance or assistance during boarding. The assessment considers vehicle access, route feasibility, and the employee's stated requirement. The proposed arrangement is communicated before activation. An unresolved administrative issue is referred to the Facilities Manager for a decision.

### 15.5 Boarding assistance
Where an employee has an approved assistance requirement, the transport manifest carries the necessary operational instruction without including unnecessary medical information. The vendor briefs the assigned driver before dispatch. The driver must allow sufficient time for safe boarding and alighting and inform the dispatch coordinator if the approved arrangement cannot be delivered. The transport desk contacts the employee to agree on an alternative before changing the confirmed boarding location.

### 15.6 Recurring medical transport
An employee requiring repeated transport for an approved medical purpose may request an allocation for no more than 7 calendar days per submission. Each required date and destination must be identified in the request. The transport desk checks route capacity and confirms the dates that can be supported. The allocation is not extended automatically. A fresh request must be submitted when the approved period expires.

### 15.7 Review of special arrangements
A special arrangement is reviewed when the employee's residential address, shift, office location, or stated functional requirement changes. The transport desk records the review date and agreed modification. The employee is informed before an existing arrangement is discontinued, except where an immediate safety concern requires suspension. The Facilities Manager decides unresolved administrative exceptions after reviewing the operational assessment.

## 16. Official and Special Trips

### 16.1 Official client-site journeys
An employee undertaking an approved official visit to a client location must obtain authorization from the head of the employee's department. The request must identify the business purpose, destination, journey date, expected departure, and employee details. The approved request is forwarded to the transport desk by email. The desk checks the authorization and vendor capacity before confirming a conveyance. Such journeys are not automatically included in the employee's ordinary office roster.

### 16.2 Training and development journeys
An employee attending an officially approved training programme at a location other than the base office may request transport through the ad-hoc process. The request must include the programme approval reference, venue, dates, and reporting schedule. The department head approves the business requirement and applicable expenditure. Each journey is confirmed separately unless a recurring allocation is expressly approved within the permitted booking horizon.

### 16.3 Inter-office journeys
A journey between Initech office locations requires a business justification and approval from the head of the employee's department. The request identifies both office addresses, participating employees, and the intended departure window. The transport desk determines whether the journey can be included in an existing dispatch or requires a separate allocation. The approved destination is recorded in the journey manifest. A change of destination requires revised authorization.

### 16.4 Conference and corporate event transport
Transport for an officially approved conference or corporate event must be requested by the organizing department. The department nominates a coordinator who provides the attendee list, venue, event timings, and estimated passenger count. The department head validates the business purpose and expenditure. The transport desk confirms vehicle capacity and boarding arrangements. Changes to the attendee list must be submitted before the vendor's final manifest is issued.

### 16.5 Airport departure journeys
An airport departure request must identify the flight departure time, terminal, collection address, and intended arrival buffer. The department head approves the official travel requirement and forwards the approved request to the transport desk by email. The desk checks the proposed schedule against the journey duration limit and confirms the allocation. The employee must verify the pickup instruction before the journey date.

### 16.6 Airport arrival journeys
An airport arrival request must identify the flight reference, expected landing time, terminal, and final destination. The employee or departmental coordinator must inform the transport desk if the flight schedule changes. The desk checks whether the existing allocation can accommodate the revised arrival. A confirmed arrival journey receives a separate dispatch reference. The employee must not assume that the original allocation remains valid after a material schedule change.

### 16.7 Special trip completion records
The vendor submits completion details for each approved special trip, including the journey reference, actual departure, final arrival, and authorized route deviations. The requesting department verifies the business purpose and passenger information. The transport desk checks the completion record against the departmental sanction before forwarding the entry for billing. Incomplete records are returned to the vendor for correction within 3 working days.

## 17. Vendors and Service Levels

### 17.1 Vendor appointment
Initech appoints transport vendors through a qualification process administered by the Facilities team. Each vendor must provide its business registration, statutory permits, insurance records, vehicle inventory, driver verification procedures, and operational escalation contacts. The Facilities team evaluates the submitted information before approving a vendor for assignments. A vendor cannot receive employee allocations until its qualification is complete. Vendor records are reviewed periodically and whenever a material compliance concern is identified.

### 17.2 Vehicle and personnel compliance
An approved vendor is responsible for ensuring that every assigned vehicle and driver satisfies the requirements in Chapter 13. The vendor maintains current documentation and provides supporting records when requested by the transport desk. A vehicle or driver with an unresolved compliance deficiency must not be assigned to a new journey. The Facilities team may request an updated compliance declaration before permitting the vendor to resume assignments. Compliance responsibility remains with the vendor throughout the service period.

### 17.3 On-time pickup performance
The vendor must achieve an on-time pickup performance target of 90% across confirmed Initech journeys in each calendar month. A pickup is considered on time when the assigned vehicle reaches the confirmed boarding point no later than the scheduled pickup time. The transport desk calculates performance using dispatch timestamps and verified journey records. A vendor missing the target must provide a written corrective action plan within 5 working days. Performance is reviewed during the monthly vendor meeting.

### 17.4 Dispatch acknowledgement
The vendor must acknowledge each confirmed dispatch instruction within 10 minutes of receipt. The acknowledgement identifies the vehicle registration reference, assigned driver, and expected arrival time. If the vendor cannot fulfil the instruction, it must immediately notify the dispatch coordinator and explain the capacity constraint. The transport desk records acknowledgement failures in the monthly service report. Repeated failures may result in restricted allocations or a formal vendor review.

### 17.5 Replacement vehicle response
When an assigned vehicle becomes unavailable before reaching the first boarding point, the vendor must notify the dispatch coordinator immediately. The vendor should propose a compliant replacement within 20 minutes of reporting the unavailability. The replacement vehicle and driver must satisfy the same documentation requirements as the original assignment. The transport desk confirms the substitution and informs affected employees. A proposed replacement is not authorized until the revised dispatch details are accepted.

### 17.6 Monthly vendor review
The transport desk conducts a monthly service review covering pickup performance, vehicle availability, employee complaints, documentation compliance, incident records, and invoice accuracy. The vendor submits its performance report by the fifth working day of the following month. Each unresolved deficiency must have an assigned owner and target completion date. The Facilities team reviews material concerns and may require a formal improvement plan. The review outcome is retained with the vendor's administrative record.

### 17.7 Service-level deductions
Where a vendor fails to meet an agreed service obligation, the transport desk records the affected journey and the relevant contractual provision. Any financial deduction is determined under the executed vendor agreement and supported by dispatch or incident records. Employees do not independently calculate or collect vendor deductions. Finance processes an approved adjustment after the transport desk validates the evidence. A disputed deduction is referred to the Facilities team for review.

### 17.8 Vendor continuity
A vendor transition must be planned so that confirmed employee allocations are not transferred using incomplete records. The outgoing vendor provides active route manifests, pending incident references, vehicle records, and unresolved invoice entries. The incoming vendor completes qualification and driver verification before receiving dispatch instructions. The transport desk validates the transferred allocations and communicates the transition date to affected employees. Unresolved service obligations remain recorded against the responsible vendor.

## 18. Billing, Charges and Reimbursement

### 18.1 Vendor billing cycle
Vendors submit a consolidated invoice for completed Initech journeys by the third working day of each month. Each invoice must identify the journey reference, service date, trip category, approved amount, completion timestamp, and applicable supporting receipt. The transport desk matches invoice entries against confirmed allocations and completion records. Entries without a valid approval reference are held for clarification. Validated invoices are forwarded to Finance under the applicable vendor agreement.

### 18.2 Billing dispute window
An employee or requesting department must raise a billing dispute within 60 calendar days of receiving the relevant invoice or charge notification. The dispute must identify the journey reference, disputed amount, and reason for disagreement. The transport desk checks the original approval, completion record, and vendor invoice. A dispute received after the prescribed period may be declined unless the Facilities Manager records an exception based on documented circumstances.

### 18.3 Departmental payment responsibility
Where an approved ad-hoc journey exceeds the company cost cap, the excess amount is billed to the employee's department. The department head receives the journey reference and supporting vendor charge information. The transport desk records the company-authorized contribution and departmental excess separately. The employee must not independently negotiate a revised company contribution with the vendor. Finance processes the departmental charge after the supporting records have been validated.

### 18.4 Supporting receipts
A vendor receipt must identify the journey date, journey reference, trip category, total amount, and applicable approved contribution. Where a charge is assigned to a department, the receipt or invoice must identify that department. The transport desk may request a corrected document when an amount or journey reference is inconsistent. The requesting department should retain the approval and completion record with the corresponding financial entry. A receipt without an identifiable journey reference may be held for clarification.

### 18.5 Emergency expenditure reimbursement
Emergency expenditure personally paid by an employee may be submitted for reimbursement when it is connected to a recorded transport emergency. The employee must submit the incident reference, expenditure details, and available supporting receipts within 5 working days. The transport desk verifies the circumstances and checks the applicable ₹2,000 medical emergency cap. Finance processes the authorized amount after administrative verification. Expenditure beyond the cap requires a separate decision from the Facilities Manager.

### 18.6 Duplicate billing
Where the same journey appears more than once in an invoice, the transport desk marks the duplicate entry and requests a corrected invoice from the vendor. The vendor must provide the correction within 3 working days. Finance must not process the duplicate entry while the correction remains pending. The original and corrected invoice references are retained together. An employee or department identifying a duplicate charge should provide the associated journey references when submitting a dispute.

### 18.7 Monthly financial reconciliation
The transport desk and Finance reconcile the monthly journey register against vendor invoices and departmental charges. The reconciliation identifies completed journeys without invoices, invoiced journeys without completion records, and amounts exceeding approved limits. Differences are assigned to the responsible administrative owner. Unresolved items are carried into the next reconciliation with their original journey references. The Facilities team receives a summary of material differences during the monthly review.

### 18.8 Departmental cost allocation
Each special journey is assigned to the department identified in its approval record. The transport desk validates the department identifier before forwarding the entry to Finance. Guest journeys are charged to the host employee's department. Weekend journeys are checked against the shift manager's approval and the applicable trip cap. An allocation cannot be transferred to another department without a recorded correction and supporting authorization.

## 19. Data, Privacy and Tracking

### 19.1 Employee transport information
Initech collects employee information required to determine eligibility, plan routes, confirm allocations, and administer transport services. This includes the employee identifier, grade, residential address, office location, shift, boarding point, and emergency contact. Access is limited to personnel whose assigned responsibilities require the information. The transport desk must not disclose a residential address to another employee merely because both employees share a route. Administrative reports should use employee identifiers wherever full personal details are unnecessary.

### 19.2 Location data retention
Journey location data is retained for 30 days from the date of the recorded journey. Access during this period is limited to authorized transport, Facilities, and investigation personnel with a valid operational purpose. Location information may be used to verify a pickup, investigate an incident, resolve a service complaint, or examine a disputed journey. At the end of the retention period, records are deleted or anonymized according to the approved information-management process, subject to a documented preservation requirement.

### 19.3 Employee access to tracking information
An employee may request information about their own confirmed journey through the transport desk. The request must identify the journey reference and date. The transport desk checks whether the relevant location record remains within the applicable retention period and whether access is authorized. Information relating to another employee's journey is not disclosed merely because the employees share a route. A tracking enquiry does not create a right to access the vendor's complete dispatch database.

### 19.4 Administrative access controls
The transport desk grants access to employee transport records according to job responsibility. A person seeking access to an employee's location history must state the operational purpose and obtain authorization from the Facilities Manager. Access events are recorded with the requester, date, record category, and reason. Unnecessary copies of employee records must not be retained in personal working folders. Access rights are reviewed every 90 days and whenever a relevant role changes.

### 19.5 Employee record correction
An employee who identifies an incorrect address, grade, contact detail, or transport profile entry must submit a correction through the HR system. The transport desk verifies the proposed change before modifying the active allocation. A correction to residential information may require a fresh route assessment. The previous value is retained only as required for an audit trail and applicable retention controls. The employee receives confirmation after the active transport profile is updated.

### 19.6 Vendor information disclosure
A vendor receives only the employee and journey information required to perform its assigned transport service. The dispatch manifest may include the employee's identifier, boarding point, destination, and necessary operational instructions. Vendors must not reuse this information for unrelated purposes or share it with unauthorized persons. A suspected unauthorized disclosure must be reported to the transport desk promptly. The Facilities team records the event and determines whether an administrative investigation is required.

### 19.7 Data preservation
When an incident, grievance, or formal investigation requires preservation of transport records, the responsible administrator identifies the relevant journey references and the reason for preservation. The transport desk locates the applicable dispatch, location, and vendor records and restricts routine deletion while the instruction remains active. The preservation record identifies an owner and review date. Records are released from preservation only after an authorized administrator confirms that the underlying matter has concluded.

### 19.8 Data access requests
Employees may submit a request for information about their own transport records by identifying the relevant journey dates and employee identifier. The transport desk checks the requester's identity and determines whether the requested information remains available. Information about another passenger is excluded unless disclosure is separately authorized. The response records the information category supplied and the date of disclosure. Requests that cannot be fulfilled because of expired retention are recorded with the reason.

## 20. Feedback, Complaints and Escalation

### 20.1 Employee feedback
Employees may submit service feedback through the approved employee communication channel using the relevant journey reference. Feedback may concern punctuality, route planning, dispatch communication, vehicle condition, or the accuracy of the boarding point. The transport desk classifies each submission and records its receipt date. General suggestions may be considered for service improvement even where they do not identify an individual journey. The desk determines whether an investigation or an operational correction is required.

### 20.2 Complaint acknowledgement
The transport desk must acknowledge a transport complaint within 3 working days of receipt. The acknowledgement confirms the complaint reference and the team responsible for the initial review. It does not constitute a finding on the merits of the complaint. The employee may provide additional supporting information against the same reference. The desk records the acknowledgement date and monitors the case until it is resolved or formally escalated.

### 20.3 Service-quality complaints
A service-quality complaint concerning punctuality, route sequence, vehicle readiness, or dispatch communication is assigned to the transport operations team. The team checks the journey manifest, vendor timestamps, and employee submission. The vendor may be asked to provide an explanation within 3 working days. The transport desk communicates the outcome and any corrective measure to the employee. Repeated complaints involving the same route are included in the monthly vendor review.

### 20.4 Driver conduct grievances
A grievance concerning driver conduct must be recorded separately from a routine punctuality complaint. The employee should identify the journey reference, approximate time, location, and nature of the reported conduct. The transport desk refers the matter to the Facilities team and vendor-management representatives. Where an immediate safety concern is alleged, the driver may be removed from further Initech assignments while the initial assessment is conducted. Serious or repeated allegations are escalated to the Facilities Manager.

### 20.5 Complaint escalation stages
The following stages govern escalation of unresolved complaints.

| Stage | Responsible role | Escalation trigger | Required record |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Transport Desk | Initial complaint received | Complaint reference |
| 2 | Transport Operations Lead | No resolution after 3 working days | Review summary |
| 3 | Facilities Manager | Material dispute remains unresolved | Escalation decision |
| 4 | Head of Facilities | Administrative implementation remains pending | Closure instruction |

### 20.6 Personal-safety grievances
A complaint involving harassment, intimidation, or personal safety must be referred promptly to the appropriate internal grievance authority. The transport desk records only the journey information necessary to identify the incident and preserve supporting records. The complaint must not be circulated through ordinary vendor performance reports. The employee may request that communication be restricted to designated grievance representatives. An immediate safety threat must be addressed through the emergency response procedure without waiting for the grievance investigation.

### 20.7 Complaint closure and appeal
A complaint is closed after the responsible team records its findings, corrective action, and communication to the employee. An employee who disagrees with the outcome may request a review by the Facilities Manager within 5 working days of receiving the closure communication. The request must identify the disputed finding or action. The Facilities Manager may request additional records from the transport desk or vendor before issuing a final administrative decision.

### 20.8 Complaint confidentiality
Complaint information is shared only with personnel responsible for investigation, administrative review, or implementation of corrective action. The transport desk records the complaint reference separately from ordinary journey-performance summaries when the matter involves personal safety. Vendor personnel receive only the information required to respond to the allegation. The Facilities team determines whether additional confidentiality restrictions are necessary. The employee is informed of the available communication channel for follow-up.

## 21. Weather and Service Disruptions

### 21.1 Weather-based service suspension
Scheduled transport is suspended when a red weather alert is issued for the operating area. The transport desk checks the alert coverage and expected validity period before issuing the service instruction. Employees are informed through the approved employee communication channel. The suspension notice identifies the affected office, shift, and review time. A department cannot independently instruct a vendor to continue a suspended service.

### 21.2 Road closure and access restrictions
A route may be suspended or diverted when a road closure, public safety instruction, or physical obstruction makes the approved journey impracticable. The vendor informs the dispatch coordinator of the affected road segment and any available alternative. The transport desk assesses whether the revised route remains within the applicable journey and boarding limits. Employees receive an updated boarding instruction before dispatch resumes. An unverified diversion is not treated as an approved route change.

### 21.3 Heavy rainfall and local flooding
Where heavy rainfall causes localized flooding without a qualifying authority alert, the dispatch coordinator assesses the affected road segments using verified operational information. A journey may be delayed, rerouted, or suspended if the route is not safely accessible. The vendor must not enter an inundated road merely to preserve the original pickup schedule. The transport desk records the affected route and reason for its decision. Employees are informed when a revised service instruction is available.

### 21.4 Public-order restrictions
A transport service may be suspended when a competent authority imposes a movement restriction affecting safe access. The transport desk records the issuing authority, affected area, effective period, and operational instruction. Vendors must comply with the restriction and must not create an alternative route that defeats the stated restriction. The Facilities Manager is informed when a suspension affects multiple offices or an entire shift cycle.

### 21.5 Employee notification during disruption
A disruption notice must identify the affected office, journey category, shift, effective time, and expected next update. Where the duration is unknown, the notice must state that service remains under review rather than provide an unsupported restoration estimate. Employees should check the official communication channel before proceeding to the assigned boarding point. The transport desk maintains a record of notices issued and the employee groups covered by each notice.

### 21.6 Service restoration
The transport desk restores service after confirming that the red weather alert has been withdrawn and the affected access route is operationally suitable. The vendor must confirm vehicle availability and provide an updated dispatch plan. Restoration is communicated before the first resumed allocation is released. A previously confirmed journey is not automatically reactivated after suspension. Employees must check for a fresh confirmation and follow the revised boarding instruction.

### 21.7 Disruption-related alternative arrangements
Where scheduled transport cannot operate because of an official suspension, the transport desk records the affected employee groups and notifies their reporting managers. Any alternative conveyance requires a separate assessment of safety, availability, and expenditure authorization. Employees must not assume that an ordinary allocation authorizes a different destination or vendor. The Facilities Manager may approve a temporary administrative arrangement where the business requirement and operating conditions justify it.

### 21.8 Disruption review
After service resumes, the transport desk reviews the affected routes, missed allocations, vendor communication, and employee notifications. The review identifies whether the original route remains suitable or requires a permanent modification. Material vendor failures are included in the monthly performance assessment. The Facilities team records significant operational findings and assigns corrective actions. Outstanding actions are monitored until their completion is confirmed.

## 22. Festivals and Peak Days

### 22.1 Festival operating calendar
The transport desk prepares a festival operating calendar using the official Initech working-day calendar and confirmed shift requirements. Departments provide expected staffing levels and shift information at least 7 working days before a major festival date. The desk uses this information to estimate route demand and vendor capacity. A festival date that is not a declared working day follows the applicable public-holiday roster restriction.

### 22.2 Peak-day demand forecast
For a declared working day expected to have unusually high transport demand, departments submit a consolidated attendance forecast to the transport desk. The forecast identifies the office, shift, employee count, and expected pickup window. The desk compares projected demand with approved vendor capacity and records any shortfall. The resulting dispatch plan is shared with departmental coordinators at least 2 working days before the operating date.

### 22.3 Festival roster confirmation
Employees must verify their attendance requirement against the published festival roster. The transport desk issues confirmed boarding details after validating the departmental shift list. An employee omitted from the approved roster must obtain a corrected shift record before transport can be added. Inclusion in a departmental attendance forecast does not independently guarantee a confirmed allocation.

### 22.4 Reduced office attendance
When an office operates with reduced staffing on a declared working day, the transport desk may consolidate compatible routes. Consolidation must preserve the applicable journey-duration and boarding-distance requirements. Employees affected by a revised sequence are informed before dispatch. A route must not be consolidated where doing so would exceed an established operational limit. The revised manifest and reason for consolidation are retained.

### 22.5 Peak-day vendor capacity
The vendor confirms the number of vehicles and verified drivers available for each approved peak-day shift. The confirmation includes known capacity constraints and a named operational coordinator. The transport desk compares the commitment with the forecast before releasing the final roster. A material shortfall is escalated to the Facilities Manager for a decision on priority allocation or a revised operating plan.

### 22.6 Festival-period schedule changes
Where the official working calendar changes after a festival roster has been published, the department coordinator informs the transport desk immediately. The desk updates the attendance basis and reviews affected allocations. Employees receive a revised confirmation or cancellation instruction. Vendor dispatch instructions must be updated separately. A calendar correction does not automatically withdraw a vehicle instruction already issued.

### 22.7 Post-festival reconciliation
Within 5 working days after a major festival operating period, the transport desk reviews actual passenger counts, cancelled journeys, dispatch failures, and vendor capacity. Departments may be asked to explain material differences between forecast and attendance. The review identifies route changes for the next planning cycle. Material vendor failures are included in the monthly service review and tracked until corrective action is completed.

## 23. Exceptions and Policy Administration

### 23.1 Exception approval authority
A deviation from ordinary transport requirements must receive written sanction from the Facilities Manager before implementation. The request identifies the employee or department, applicable requirement, business justification, proposed duration, expenditure impact, and operational safeguards. The transport desk records the decision against the relevant request reference. A manager's informal instruction does not constitute exception approval. Emergency handling remains subject to the specific emergency procedures in these guidelines.

### 23.2 Temporary exception requests
A temporary exception must specify its start date and end date. The requesting department explains why ordinary eligibility, roster, or booking arrangements cannot meet the documented requirement. The Facilities Manager may approve, reject, or request additional information. An approved exception applies only to the named employee, office, date range, and business purpose. It does not establish a general entitlement for other employees in the same department.

### 23.3 Permanent deviations
A request for a permanent deviation must include an operational assessment and an explanation of why a temporary arrangement is insufficient. The Facilities Manager reviews the proposal with the Facilities team and Human Resources. A request that changes a general policy requirement must be processed as a formal guideline amendment rather than maintained as an informal exception. Approved amendments receive a version identifier and effective date.

### 23.4 Emergency administrative decisions
Where an immediate operational situation prevents prior written approval, the transport desk may take only the minimum action necessary to protect employee safety or facilitate a medical emergency. The action, reason, affected employee, and expenditure are recorded as soon as practicable. The Facilities Manager reviews the record on the next working day. This procedure does not establish a general waiver of eligibility, booking, or cost requirements.

### 23.5 Guideline ownership and review
The Facilities team owns these guidelines and is responsible for maintaining the approved version. The transport desk administers daily operations and proposes changes based on operational evidence. These guidelines are reviewed every six months or earlier when a material regulatory or operational change occurs. Proposed amendments must identify their rationale and impact. Only an authorized policy owner may issue a revised controlled version.

### 23.6 Conflicting instructions
Where a departmental instruction conflicts with an approved allocation or these guidelines, the transport desk refers the issue to the Facilities team for interpretation. The applicable safety requirement takes priority while clarification is pending. Employees and vendors must not treat an unverified message as a formal amendment. The interpretation and resulting instruction are recorded against the affected journey or policy reference.

### 23.7 Employee acknowledgement
The Facilities team publishes the current guidelines through the approved employee information system. Employees acknowledge the applicable transport requirements when registering for company transport and when a material revision is issued. The transport desk may direct employees to the relevant section when resolving a request. Acknowledgement confirms receipt of the guidelines and does not replace a separate approval or booking requirement.

### 23.8 Administrative records
The Facilities team maintains the controlled guideline version, approval history, review dates, and amendment records. The transport desk maintains operational rosters, booking references, dispatch records, and exception decisions. Each record must identify the responsible administrative role and the date of the relevant action. Records are made available to authorized reviewers when required. A proposed change remains a draft until it receives formal approval and an effective date.

## 24. Roles and Contacts

### 24.1 Facilities team
The Facilities team owns these guidelines and coordinates their formal review. It maintains the approved version and evaluates proposed amendments. The team reviews material vendor-performance concerns and administrative escalations referred by the transport desk. It ensures that the transport framework remains aligned with approved operating requirements. Routine vehicle dispatch and employee booking confirmation remain responsibilities of the transport desk.

### 24.2 Transport desk
The transport desk manages registration verification, route planning, booking validation, vendor dispatch, journey monitoring, complaint intake, and invoice reconciliation. It maintains the official transport roster and communicates confirmed changes. The desk records exceptions and escalates matters requiring formal sanction. Employees should provide their journey reference when requesting assistance so that the relevant dispatch and allocation records can be retrieved.

### 24.3 Department head
The head of the employee's department approves ad-hoc journeys and official airport journeys. The department head also authorizes guest journeys and validates departmental expenditure where the applicable trip category requires it. Approval must be recorded before the request is forwarded to the transport desk. A department head cannot independently dispatch a vehicle or waive an operational safety requirement.

### 24.4 Shift manager
The shift manager confirms rostered weekend duty and approves the applicable weekend transport allocation. The role verifies that the employee appears on the approved shift list before the transport desk prepares the manifest. The shift manager also communicates corrections to the shift roster. Approval of a shift assignment does not itself establish that a transport allocation has been confirmed.

### 24.5 Facilities Manager
The Facilities Manager provides the formal exception sanction required by these guidelines and reviews serious operational escalations. The role may approve, reject, or request revision of a documented deviation. The Facilities Manager reviews unresolved grievances, material incident findings, and operational expenditure exceptions. Decisions must identify the employee or department, request reference, effective period, and any conditions attached to the sanction.

### 24.6 Human Resources
Human Resources maintains the employee record used to establish grade and employment status. It processes address and employment changes through the HR system and provides authoritative confirmation where a record is disputed. HR receives no-show matters referred under Chapter 7 and participates in formal employee grievance processes. Human Resources does not independently amend vendor dispatch instructions.

### 24.7 Vendor dispatch coordinator
The vendor dispatch coordinator manages vehicle assignment, driver verification, dispatch acknowledgement, route execution, and operational substitutions. The coordinator communicates service failures and journey incidents to the transport desk. The coordinator must follow the approved manifest and cannot independently change an employee's destination or boarding point. Material dispatch changes require an updated instruction.

### 24.8 Employee responsibilities
Employees must maintain accurate HR records, submit transport requests within the applicable booking window, and use confirmed allocations. They must verify the assigned boarding location and report material discrepancies. Employees must also submit incident notifications and complaints through the designated channels. An employee is responsible for checking that a submitted request has reached confirmed status before relying on the requested journey.
