# Acme Corp Employee Transport Policy
<!-- company_id: acme | version: v2 | effective_date: 2026-10-01 -->

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This policy sets out how Acme Corp provides home-to-office transport for its employees. It aims to give every eligible employee a safe, punctual and fair commute. The transport desk at each Acme office administers this policy on behalf of the Administration department. This version replaces the policy dated 1 January 2026.

### 1.2 Applicability
This policy applies to all permanent employees of Acme Corp working from an Acme office in India. Contract staff and interns are covered only where their engagement letter says so. Where this policy conflicts with a local law, the local law prevails.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Eligibility criteria
Employees in grades L1 to L5 are eligible for company transport. The employee's registered home address must be more than 3 km from the office by road. Employees in grade L6 and above are not eligible and receive a conveyance allowance through payroll instead.

### 2.2 Change of address
An employee who moves home must update their address on the transport portal at least 3 working days before the change takes effect. Eligibility is re-checked against the new address. Routes are re-planned every Monday.

## 3. Night Shift

### 3.1 Shift slot timetable
Transport is planned only for the shift slots below. The drop time is when the cab leaves the office.

| Shift | Login | Logout | Drop departs | Escort for women employees |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Early | 6:00 AM | 3:00 PM | 3:15 PM | No |
| General | 9:00 AM | 6:00 PM | 6:15 PM | No |
| Late general | 11:00 AM | 8:00 PM | 8:15 PM | Yes |
| Afternoon | 2:00 PM | 11:00 PM | 11:15 PM | Yes |
| Evening | 3:30 PM | 12:00 AM | 12:30 AM | Yes |
| Night | 10:00 PM | 7:00 AM | 7:15 AM | Yes, for the 9:00 PM pickup |

### 3.2 Security escort for women employees
A trained security guard travels in the cab on every drop that leaves the office after 8:00 PM when a woman employee is on board. The same applies to pickups before 6:00 AM. The guard sits in the front seat and stays in the cab until the last woman employee has been dropped.

The transport desk verifies the guard's identity card at the start of every trip. If no guard is available, the cab does not leave and the desk arranges a replacement. A woman employee is never the first pickup or the last drop on a route unless a guard is on board.

### 3.3 Last drop slot
The last scheduled drop leaves the office at 12:30 AM. Employees who work beyond the last drop slot need an ad-hoc trip under section 6.

## 4. Booking

### 4.1 Booking cut-off
Employees must book or change a scheduled pickup or drop at least 2 hours before the trip on the transport portal. Requests made after the cut-off are treated as ad-hoc trips.

### 4.2 Cancellations
A trip can be cancelled without penalty up to 1 hour before the scheduled time. A trip cancelled later than that, or not cancelled at all, counts as a no-show under section 7.

## 5. Weekends and Holidays

### 5.1 Weekend pickups
Saturday pickups and drops are available without any approval. Employees book them on the transport portal by Friday 6:00 PM. Transport is not provided on Sundays.

### 5.2 Public holidays
No transport is provided on public holidays. If a business unit declares a holiday as a working day, transport runs as on a Saturday.

## 6. Ad-hoc and Airport Trips

### 6.1 Approval for ad-hoc and airport trips
Ad-hoc trips, including trips to or from the airport for official travel, need two approvals before the trip: first the employee's reporting manager, then the transport desk. Both approvals are recorded on the transport portal. The trip is subject to the caps in section 8.

## 7. No-shows

### 7.1 No-show policy
A no-show is recorded when an employee is not at the pickup point within 5 minutes of the cab's arrival, or misses a drop without cancelling. The first no-show in a calendar month leads to a warning. A second no-show in the same month carries a fine of ₹200, recovered through payroll.

### 7.2 No-show penalty schedule
No-shows are counted per calendar month.

| No-shows in a month | Action | Notified |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warning | Employee |
| 2 | ₹200 fine | Employee, reporting manager |
| 3 | ₹500 fine and transport suspended for 1 week | Employee, reporting manager |
| 4 or more | Transport suspended for 1 month | Employee, reporting manager, HR |

## 8. Costs

### 8.1 Cost cap
The company pays up to ₹2,000 for a single ad-hoc trip. Any amount above the cap is recovered from the employee through payroll.

### 8.2 Cost caps by trip type

| Trip type | Cap per trip | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Ad-hoc | ₹2,000 | Reporting manager and transport desk |
| Airport | ₹3,000 | Reporting manager and transport desk |
| Saturday | ₹1,500 | No approval needed |
| Guest | ₹3,500 | Vice President |
| Late stay beyond last drop | ₹1,000 | Transport desk |

## 9. Guest Travel

### 9.1 Guest travel
Transport for visiting guests, such as candidates or client visitors, is allowed with approval from a Vice President. The host employee books the trip and is responsible for the guest during the trip.
