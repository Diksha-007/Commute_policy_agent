# Globex Industries Commute Policy
<!-- company_id: globex | version: v1 | effective_date: 2026-04-01 -->

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose
Globex Industries provides shared cab transport so that employees can reach the office safely and on time. This policy is owned by Human Resources and operated by the transport desk. It describes who can use company transport and the rules that apply.

### 1.2 Applicability
This policy covers all full-time Globex employees based in Globex offices in India. It does not cover employees on deputation to client sites, who follow the client's transport arrangements.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Eligibility criteria
Employees of all grades are eligible for company transport. The employee's registered home address must be more than 8 km from the office by road. Employees living 8 km or closer are not provided transport.

### 2.2 Change of address
Address changes must be submitted to the transport desk at least 5 working days in advance. Eligibility is re-checked against the new address. Routes are re-planned on the 1st and 15th of each month.

## 3. Night Shift

### 3.1 Shift slot timetable
Transport is planned only for the shift slots below. The drop time is when the cab leaves the office.

| Shift | Login | Logout | Drop departs | Escort for women employees |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | 7:00 AM | 4:00 PM | 4:15 PM | No |
| General | 9:30 AM | 6:30 PM | 6:45 PM | No |
| Mid | 12:00 PM | 9:00 PM | 9:15 PM | No |
| Afternoon | 1:30 PM | 10:30 PM | 11:00 PM | Yes |
| Night | 11:00 PM | 8:00 AM | 8:15 AM | Yes, for the 10:00 PM pickup |

### 3.2 Security escort for women employees
A security escort is provided on drops that leave the office after 10:00 PM when a woman employee is on board. Drops at or before 10:00 PM do not carry an escort. Pickups before 6:00 AM with a woman employee also carry an escort.

The escort is a guard from the security agency contracted by Globex. Women employees can track their cab live in the Globex mobile app and share the trip with a family member. After each night drop, the transport desk calls to confirm that the employee has reached home.

### 3.3 Last drop slot
The last scheduled drop leaves the office at 11:00 PM. No scheduled transport is available after that time.

## 4. Booking

### 4.1 Booking cut-off
Bookings and changes must be made at least 6 hours before the trip through the Globex mobile app. Late requests are not accepted for scheduled trips.

### 4.2 Cancellations
Trips can be cancelled up to 2 hours before the scheduled time. Later cancellations are recorded as no-shows.

## 5. Weekends and Holidays

### 5.1 Weekend pickups
Company transport is not provided on Saturdays or Sundays. Employees working on a weekend arrange their own travel.

### 5.2 Public holidays
On holidays that Globex declares as working days, transport runs as on a normal weekday. No transport is provided on other public holidays.

## 6. Ad-hoc and Airport Trips

### 6.1 Approval for ad-hoc and airport trips
Ad-hoc trips and airport trips for official travel must be approved by the employee's HR business partner. Approval requests are raised in the Globex mobile app at least one day in advance. Medical emergencies are the only exception and are handled directly by the transport desk.

## 7. No-shows

### 7.1 No-show policy
An employee who is not at the pickup point within 10 minutes of the cab's arrival is marked as a no-show. Three no-shows in a calendar month suspend the employee's transport for two weeks.

### 7.2 No-show penalty schedule
No-shows are counted per calendar month.

| No-shows in a month | Action | Notified |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logged, no action | None |
| 2 | Reminder message | Employee |
| 3 | Transport suspended for 2 weeks | Employee, HR business partner |
| 4 or more | Referred to HR for review | Employee, HR business partner |

## 8. Costs

### 8.1 Cost cap
Globex pays up to ₹1,200 for a single ad-hoc trip. Amounts above the cap must be paid by the employee directly to the vendor.

### 8.2 Cost caps by trip type

| Trip type | Cap per trip | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Ad-hoc | ₹1,200 | HR business partner |
| Airport | ₹2,000 | HR business partner |
| Medical emergency | ₹1,500 | Transport desk |
| Late stay beyond last drop | ₹600 | HR business partner |
